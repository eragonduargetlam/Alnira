## Briefing
	Ulberic and Pandora can provide the following information:
	
- An orchid was stolen from their community, located a couple days walk away from the city. It was not taken by accident: its guardians were frozen immobile with magic, and the flower was gone when they finally freed themselves. A sprit seer managed to locate the orchid's essence: it is held within the [[Petal and Thorn]].

- Once [[Ulberic]] and [[Pandora]] arrived in town, wards had already been set up to prevent sprites from entering the building. The magic in these wards, as well as the aura surrounding the greenhouse, seems shockingly similar to fey magic, which the sprites find alarming.

- While investigating in the city, they found out that while a popular spot for leisurely visits, the building also houses the headquarters, labs and main selling location of a luxury brand focusing on cosmetics and self improvement.

- A garden party gala will be held in the building tonight to celebrate the launch of a new line of products, which hopefully should work in the party's advantage. In the midst of a party, employees will likely be busier than usual. Attendees will natually require fancy attire, which the sprites can procure for the party.

- It is of utmost importance that the Orchid is returned to the sprites intact and as urgently as possible. The Orchid is their sole concern: anything else that the characters find and take during the mission is theirs to keep.

#### Optional event: Secret Bound

Characters may insist on knowing what is so special about this orchid. The sprites might be convince with some form of guarantee before spilling the secret. If a character truly wishes to know, they can enter a fey bargain with [[Pandora]] by giving her a lock of their hair, after which she will reveal what is held within the orchid.

A character who enters this bargain sees a purple ring form on the skin around their wrists. As long as the deal is active, sharing the secret will cause them to take 6d6 direct damage on a failed **DC 15 Instinct reaction roll**, or half on a successful one. Additionally, any damage they deal to the orchid is copied to them.

This fey bargain ends once the orchid is returned safely to [[Ulberic]] and [[Pandora]]. The deal also ends if the lock of hair is destroyed, if the fey that issued the bargain dies, or chooses to release the character.

## Sneaking Weapons into the Gala

Most aristocrats and socialites would consider bringing bloodied weapons to an upscale event as a dreadful social faux-pas... and an immediate threat to their safety. Characters entering the Gala with visible weapons or armor will immediately arouse suspicion. Security guards and employees keep and eye out, and will address those who break the rule as soon as they are noticed, escorting them out should they refuse to comply and leave their weapons in the employee's care for the duration of the event.

The party can circumvent this issue in several ways. Ceremonial or official weapons and gear are permitted for high rank individuals such as decorated officers. A character can convince employees that they qualify for this exemption with a successful **DC 15 presence action roll**, so long as their gear looks the part. Alternatively, the party may sneak weapons into the event by hiding them in their gala attire and succeeding on an Agility action roll against the employee or security guard.
