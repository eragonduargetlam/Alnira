### 1. The Abandoned Apartment Job

- **Setup:** Indigo or Orion gets a message from an old contact about a lucrative but odd job. The only instruction: meet at an abandoned apartment in [[Velcairn]]’s older quarter.
    
- **Twist:** The client won’t meet directly; instead, the apartment is where [[Ulberic]] & [[Pandora]] are waiting. They reveal that Destiny’s gala tomorrow night is a front for unveiling a stolen Fey orchid as part of her new product line.
    
- **Funnel:** Players walk in already primed for the heist angle, and the sprites supply the blueprints and gadgets.
    

---

### 2. The Empty Seat at the Tavern

- **Setup:** A tavern regular (hedge-wizard, mercenary, guild courier) hasn’t been seen in days.
    
- **Twist:** The barkeep mutters they were last overheard bragging about “sneaking into Destiny’s big gala tomorrow” — then vanished after a late-night visit to the [[Petal and Thorn]].
    
- **Funnel:** Following up leads the party to [[Ulberic]] & [[Pandora]], who explain the truth: the missing soul got too close to the Orchid theft and was silenced. The gala is their only shot at recovery.
    

---

### 3. The Disrupted Festival Stall

- **Setup:** In a busy market square, a vendor’s stall bursts into chaos — flowers overgrowing, herbs wilting, birds dive-bombing displays.
    
- **Twist:** Onlookers whisper it’s _fey mischief_, stirred up by [[Destiny]]’s preparations for her gala product launch. “Sprite curses” are the rumor of the day.
    
- **Funnel:** The party finds [[Ulberic]] & [[Pandora]] at the edge of the crowd, trying to deflect blame. If engaged, they reveal their true motive: [[Destiny]] stole their [[Orchid]], and tomorrow’s launch will expose its power.
    

---

### 4. The Patron’s Peculiar Request

- **Setup:** A wealthy client discreetly asks the party to “acquire” an exotic bloom rumored to be unveiled at Destiny’s gala tomorrow night.
    
- **Twist:** Casing the Petal and Thorn reveals heavy wards and Fey-like magic. [[Ulberic]] & [[Pandora]] cross paths with the party and cut in: _the bloom isn’t an ornament — it’s our stolen [[Orchid]]._
    
- **Funnel:** The sprites redirect the job from petty theft to a full-blown heist, equipped with their stolen blueprints and gadgets.
    

---

### 5. The PitchKettle Approach

- **Setup:** In the [[PitchKettle]] tavern, a cloaked figure hops onto the PCs’ table, no taller than a mug of ale: [[Ulberic]], a sprite, desperate and blunt.
    
- **Twist:** He lays it bare — [[Destiny]] stole an [[Orchid]] from their grove, and tomorrow’s gala is when she’ll unveil it as the crown jewel of her product line.
    
- **Funnel:** With no time to waste, Ulberic pushes the party into action, promising Fey favors and strange tools if they help pull off the heist.
    

---

### 6. The Whispered Orchid

- **Setup:** Anywhere the party lingers — guild hall, bathhouse, market — they overhear gossip about [[Destiny]]’s gala tomorrow night.
    
- **Twist:** People describe her new product as something “impossibly rare, glowing like moonlight, more alive than any bloom.” The whispers keep circling back to Fey origins.
    
- **Funnel:** If the party digs deeper, [[Ulberic]] & [[Pandora]] surface to confirm the truth: the Orchid is theirs, and they need mortal help to reclaim it before Destiny unveils it.

Can kidnap player(s) but not ideal.
