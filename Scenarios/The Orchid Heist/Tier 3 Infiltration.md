## Staff only areas overview

The following applies by default in this tier:

#### People
- On the ground floor, the staff only areas are deserted except for the kitchen, in which 2 [[employees]] on waitstaff duty frequently refill their trays.

- [[Dr. Nathaniel Mirville]] works in the manufacturing lab on the first floor. Employees in the shop send requests up via the dumbwaiter, and Nathaniel sends the appropriate products back to them. If he crosses the threshold into the lounge, the staircase or the highly restricted areas, he marks a stress for every step until he returns to the labs.

- A [[Security Guards]] patrols through the labs.

- While employees may be persuaded or intimidated before resorting to combat, [[Security Guards]] immediately attack any intruders found in the labs area, unless they are wearing **lab coats**, tricking the guard.

#### Infrastructure
- The storage and locker rooms on the ground floor contain items that may be useful: 
		- 6 **lab coats**
		- A pitchfork that may be used as a **spear**.

- In the labs, 3 **alert bells** are fixed on different walls. Red dots indicate their location on the [[Orchid GM map]]:
![[Pasted image 20250917165754.png]]
	Each bears a sign with the words "alert protocol". Ringing these bells alerts [[Destiny]] and all her [[Security Guards]] with a ping in their mind. They are mostly used to alert when a specimen becomes hostile in the labs, but staff may also use them in case of intruders.

- At the end of a long corridor to the west of the plant healing tub, a door is marked with the words "highly restricted access". Unlike the other staff-only areas, this door and the western door into [[Destiny]]'s office do not open with a staff key. Instead, a passage gem must be pressed into a slot to the side of the door.

## Encounters in the Labs

The truth of [[Destiny]]'s labs is far from the façade that she curated. The labs have no windows, and the ingredients are disgusting at best, sentient and in pain at worst. Some plants are plagued with pulsing purple pustules, while others have been fused with pieces of flesh and mutated into grotesque distortions of themselves.

## The Trapped Researcher

[[Dr. Nathaniel Mirville]], head of labs, can be found in the manufacturing lab. Not having seen anyone other than his coworkers since his deal with [[Destiny]] 4 years ago, the party's arrival catches him by surprise. If they are hostile to him, he seeks out a [[Security Guards]] to deal with them, or runs to the nearest **alert bell**.
Otherwise, desperate after years of entrapment, he recognizes the group's arrival as his best chance to finally defeat [[Destiny]]. He can help by healing them (1hp or 1 stress) and by providing the following information:
- [[Destiny]] is a hag, and uses her products to prey on insecurities and cultivate her clients' self-hatred.
- Beyond the guards who are not quite people anymore, all the employees are trapped to some extent. Nathaniel can explain the circumstances of his own deal, but does not know that he can be released simply by someone reading his **manuscript**. 
- Her lair is located under the building, and is accessible via an elevator in the highly restricted area. All guards carry the **passage gem** necessary to enter the zone. If asked about the [[Orchid]], he can confirm that it will likely be there..

This information can also be found in a journal that he keeps in his bedroom, through the door at the far end of his office. A **shortsword** smuggled for him by an employee is hidden under Nathaniel's bed, which he may offer to the party.

## Destiny's Office

Searching the room's drawers and cabinets reveals **Destiny's Herbarium**, a spare **passage gem**, and a ledger keeping track of the debt racked up by [[Destiny]]'s most high profile clients. Some of these debts are not to be repaid in gold, but in other forms of sacrifice: a patch of their skin, a secret, a vial of blood, etc. A character standing in plain sigh on the balcony will be spotted by the crowd of attendees below.

## Progressing to [[Tier 4 The Lair]]

Tier 3 concludes when the party enters [[Destiny]]'s secret elevator. It is located within the highly restricted access area, which can b e unlocked by pressing a **passage gem** into the slot near the doors. Alternatively, characters can disable the mechanism of the gem slot with a successful **DC 15 Finesse** action roll, or force them open with a successful **DC 20 Strength** action roll.
