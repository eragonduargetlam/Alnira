## The Stolen Orchid

Destiny keeps her most precious belongings in this small study, including the stolen [[Orchid]]. It sits in a pot in the far end of the room, under a glass bell jar. Taking a closer look at the flower reveals small iridescent orbs faintly glowing beneath the petals. Characters who were unaware of the orchid's true nature and succeed on a DC 15 Knowledge action roll can recognize the pollen as necessary for sprite eggs to hatch. Characters searching this study can also find a small chest containing 1 bag of gold, gems and jewelry.

## Claiming the Orchid's Power

A crystal alembic, a mortar and pestle, and an opened letter rest on a table near the orchid:
	_Destiny dear,
		The audacity in your letter was a risk. Lucky for you, the success you have found in your recent endeavors shows that risk suits you well. If nothing else, then at least for my own curiosity and amusement, I will grant your request. For each dose, crush three orbs of pollen in a mortar, and gather the resulting paste in my alembic: it will do the lion's share of the work, and accelerate the process. Fold your paste with the tallow before distilling, and keep the heat high. Your oil of youth and beauty can be applied on the skin, or swallowed while still liquid, it's all the same in the end. I look forward to seeing the fruits of your labour, and what you will do with a product of such potency and rarity. I would urge you not to forget this favour."_
Following these steps and distilling three pollen orb creates a dose of youth oil. Once consumed, it grants the character a permanent +1 presence and allows them to reset their apparent age to that of their choice. There are enough for 5 doses. 

## Fighting Destiny

Unless all her husk minions have been defeated, [[Destiny]] never walks into a fight alone. If caught unaccompanied and entirely by surprise, she tries to buy some time in order to telepathically summon her husk minions. Hiding her anger behind a diplomatic smile, [[Destiny]] first attempts to bargain. She consults her [[Vicious mirrors]] for any knowledge she may use in order to offer a targeted deal. For example, an ill character may be offered a cure. Without any specific information, she offers each character a dose of youth oil, in exchange for a fey bargain, preventing them from speaking of the true nature of her operations without talking 6d6 direct damage.

Should combat occur, [[Destiny]] stays close to her husk minions, using her **Girlboss** trait to let them take hits in her stead. She uses her spell to incapacitate or restrain her opponents, making them more vulnerable to her and her husk minion's attacks. If reduced to a third of her total hit points, she prioritizes getting away alive over saving her cover and her business. She uses her invisible passage trait to flee safely.

Depending on the spells that the party disabled in the Lair, [[Dr. Nathaniel Mirville]] or [[Ulberic]] and [[Pandora]] may join and help in combat. Nathaniel's priority is helping the party defeat [[Destiny]], while the sprites' priority is securing the Orchid.

#### Optional Event: The Miracle of Life

The pollen is reaching potency, causing the flower to bloom and glow. These orbs are filled with magic, causing fey energy to flood the area. Roll on the table below at the beginning of each GM spotlight as a free action to determine an environmental effect caused by the orchid blooming.

| 1d6 result | Environmental effect                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1          | A harmless sensory effect fills the room: echoing children's laughter, faintly glowing wisps floating in the air, or illusory butterflies                          |
| 2          | For the next minute, all creatures within the room can now understand plants. The vines on the walls are repeatedly screaming "WATER"                              |
| 3          | One random creature in the room can no longer speak until the end of their next turn. Whenever they try, butterflies escape their mouth.                           |
| 4          | The creature nearest to the orchid regains 1 HP                                                                                                                    |
| 5          | A random creature is turned invisible (considered hidden until they act)                                                                                           |
| 6          | A glowing dust envelops the area in melee around the orchid, revealing invisible and hidden creatures and conferring advantage on attacks against those afflicted. |
