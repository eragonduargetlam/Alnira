## Destiny's Secret Lair

The magical elevator leads into a damp stone basement faintly smelling of rot. Some of the walls are covered with vines and small carnivorous plants, while others are lined with shelves bearing odd jars, bottles and other containers.

[[Destiny]]'s active spells are found in this room. Except for the Sleeping Room, which guards the path to the Vault, dispelling any of these will alert [[Destiny]]. If a spell is broken, the torches in the room turn red, and [[Destiny]]'s voice is heard speaking through the mouths of the carnivorous plants in the room.

## Vicious Mirrors

Small mirror shards cover the eastern wall. They display the reflections of the greenhouse visitors or party members whose insecurities were uncovered by the [[Vicious mirrors]]. Pulsing silver veins link these shards to a centerpiece ornate mirror, displaying [[Destiny]]'s reflection.

The [[Vicious mirrors]] are linked to [[Destiny]]'s mind via this centerpiece mirror. With them, [[Destiny]] can think of a person and telepathically interrogate the mirrors. She learns all information that the mirrors collected on the chosen individual. If the centerpiece mirror is shattered, the spell and link are broken.

## The Manuscript

Sound of the room, a small chest contains **Nathaniels manuscript** and 3 vials of **liquid confidence** which until the next long rest, grants a +1 to presence reaction rolls.

4 years ago, hoping for [[Destiny]] to sponsor and publish his research, [[Dr. Nathaniel Mirville]] pitched **liquid confidence**, a remedy that he created to ease self-doubt. She tricked him into a deal: _until the world sees this writing, this lab will be your home and study"_. Soon after, she stole his work

Nathaniel is now trapped, unable to leave the labs. A character reeading Nathaniel's manuscript, there for _seeing his writing_, breaks the spell and sets him free.

## Freeing the Truth

A painting on the west wall of the chamber depicts the entire staff team of [[Petal and Thorn]]. On the face of each staff member except [[Destiny]], [[Dr. Nathaniel Mirville]] and the [[Security Guards]], a lock of hair is pinned.

Upon hiring each employee, [[Destiny]] asked them to agree to a confidentiality clause, by entering a fey bargain with her. Should they reveal anything about [[Destiny]] and her brand's true nature, they suffer 3 hp of psychic damage, almost assuredly killing them.

Similar to the fey bargain offered by [[Pandora]], burning these locks of hair will release the employees from their deal.

## The Sigil Wards

A full length mirror stands in the corner of the room. A sigil identical to the one on the cover of **Destiny's Herbarium** is painted on its surface with a thick ichor mixed with red petals. A bowl containing the same mixture, and a large glass bottle containing only the ichor, sit on a side table. Small jars of dried petals, each labeled with the name of a different plant from **Destiny's Herbarium**, are lined up on a nearby shelf. A successful **DC 10 knowledge** action roll reveals that the red petals are dried Lazare orchids. Having found **Destiny's Herbarium** grants advantage on this check.

The sigil on the mirror creates wards around the building, barring entry or exit, while the flowers mixed into the ichor determine which creatures are subjected to this spell. Since Lazare orchid petals are currently used, sprites cannot enter the building. Should the mirror be wiped, the wards are dispelled. In the background, [[Pandora]] and [[Ulberic]], sensing the shift in magic, rush inside the greenhouse and attempt to join the group.

A character may set up new wards by mixing the flowers of their choice with the ichor and drawing the sigil again on the mirror. The list of creatures and their corresponding flowers can be found in **Destiny's Herbarium**.

## The Sleeping Room

At the far end of the room, a rotting wooden door leads into a dark space. Instead of walls, floor or ceiling, the room is made of an ever so slightly moving mass of moss, meat and sinew. A raspy, cooing voice hums a wordless lullaby, accompanied by squelching fleshy sounds. It guards the path to [[Destiny]]'s most precious belongings.

Any attempt to create or bring light into the space deeply upsets the room, which begins to shriek and slam its appendages against the offending character, and attempting to disable the source of light. The character must succeed a DC 15 Agility reaction roll or suffer 3d6 physical damage. As soon as the space is plunged into darkness again, the room calms down and resumes its lullaby.

#### Solution
	The room is not inherently hostile, but has been cursed by [[Destiny]]: light causes it great fear and pain. Acting similarly to a scared baby, it soothes itself with a lullaby. The party can gain the creature's trust by speaking softly to it, humming along to its lullaby, or by simply existing peacefully in its space and letting it get familiar with them. The room will then rearrange its own structure to let them through. Alternatively, the room is defeated if it recieves a total 12hp (Thresholds 6, 18, Diff 10). Should [[Destiny]] die, the curse ends and hte room becomes harmless.

## Progressing to Tier 5

Tier 4 concludes when the party enters the Vault either by solving the Sleeping Room puzzle or by defeating it in combat. Alternatively, should [[Destiny]] be alerted and on her way before the players enter the Vault, tier 4 concludes upon her arrival in the lair.
