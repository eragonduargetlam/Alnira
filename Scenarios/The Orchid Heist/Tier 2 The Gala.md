## Preying on Insecurities

Destiny has set up enchantments to influence visitors and prey on their self image related fears.

- The mirrors in the powder room, the lobby, the lounge and the towers are [[Vicious mirrors]], enchanted to use a person's fear against them. A character failing **DC15 Presence or Knowledge reaction roll** while near the mirrors sees their reflection slightly distorted to match their main insecurity. Only the affected character sees this distortion.

- Each time a character enters the lobby, the main greenhouse, the shop or the towers for the first time, they must make a **DC15 Presence or Knowledge reaction roll**. On a failure, [[Destiny]]'s [[Vicious mirrors]] learn the character's greatest insecurity, and the character can no longer be targeted by this effect. Succeeding on this reaction shows a normal reflection, and grants advantage on the next roll against this effect.

- Consuming drinks or pastries from the [[Cafe menu]] gives a disadvantage on these saves.

## [[Destiny]]'s Security Agents

3 [[Security Guards]] patrol on the ground floor. One is stationed in the shop, and the other two circle through the greenhouses. The 4th and last security guard stayed in the labs. All carry a [[staff key]] and a [[passage gem]] in their vest pocket.

These former clients of [[Destiny]] had to forfeit their soul to pay the debt accumulated for the "cosmetic treatments". A sickly plant-based poison pulses through their veins, keeping them alive. Now nearly mindless husks, they obey the commands of [[Destiny]], who can telepathically speak to them, though they cannot respond.

Suits, gloves and golden masks cover their skin. Though instructed never to remove these while in the public areas of the greenhouse, they may do so if circumstances require them to bite or claw.

They do not speak to visitors unless absolutely necessary. Wen they do, it is in very few words, and with a raspy, gutteral voice.

## Rumors Among the Attendees

Nearly a hundred attendees are present during the gala. They are all relatively well-off individuals, with curated appearances and well rehearsed manners. They can be found in any of the greenhouses, in the shop, in the lobby or in the powder room.

## Progressing to [[Tier 3 Infiltration]]

Tier 2 concludes when the party enters the staff restricted areas, either via the staff break room, the kitchens, the lounge, the shop's dumbwaiter or the storage room. This can be done in a variety of ways, including:
- Stealing a staff key from employees.
- Lockpicking any of the doors to the staff areas
- Sneaking through the kitchen doors from the café
- Intimidating, bribing or persuading an employee
- Shrinking in size to sneakily climb through the shop's dumbwaiter
