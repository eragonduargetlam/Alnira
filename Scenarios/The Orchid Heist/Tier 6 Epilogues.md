[[Destiny]]'s defeat causes a scandal in town. As the employees are free to finally talk of what truly happened behind the scenes, the ripple on some of her clients' reputations are disastrous. The supply of products abruptly stops, but withdrawal symptoms will eventually fade away. Seeing daylight for the first time in years, and eternally grateful to the party, [[Dr. Nathaniel Mirville]] works to rebuild his life. He does his best to provide assistance whenever he can. His knowledge as a researcher, and the healing properties of the remedies he creates could be of use.

Escorted by [[Ulberic]] and [[Pandora]], the orchid arrives home where the tale of the party's good deed spreads fast. Should the group ever need the assistance or knowledge of the sprite tribe, they will be ready to answer the call.

But if the party chose to join [[Destiny]]'s side and enter a deal with her, her wealthy influence and resources make her a very powerful ally. Though her patronage comes a price (her goal remains to corrupt), she pays well on quests to fetch new ingredients or silence stray witnesses.

Characters who chose to claim the pollen's power for themselves will have to face the wrath of the tribe. Soon, [[Ulberic]] and [[Pandora]] will relate the news to their whole community, who swear to return tenfold the harm that was done to their children.

### **Dawnbreaker Synod Countdown Clock**

_(0–6 ticks → from “unaware” to “active persecution”)_

- **If Destiny Falls (party defeats her):**
    
    - _Take:_ The Synod is pleased to see corruption uprooted. They don’t love that outsiders solved it first, but it’s not damning.
        
    - _Clock impact:_ **0–1 tick (GM’s call).** If the party handled it cleanly, no rise at all. Only tick it if they use obviously heretical tools or flaunt the Synod’s authority.
        
- **If the Orchid Is Returned (party sides with sprites):**
    
    - _Take:_ Publicly, this looks like a good deed. Privately, any bond with the sprites is worrisome. The closer the party leans into “allies of the tribe,” the more it stirs suspicion.
        
    - _Clock impact:_ **0–2 ticks.** No increase if they simply restore balance; +1 or +2 only if they openly embrace the sprites as patrons/friends.
        
- **If the Party Joins Destiny:**
    
    - _Take:_ This sets off alarms. Even if Destiny is powerful, working with her looks like consorting with corruption.
        
    - _Clock impact:_ **+2–3 ticks.** Depends on how openly the party flaunts her patronage. A secret deal might only be +2, while public loyalty is a +3.
        
- **If the Party Steals the Pollen:**
    
    - _Take:_ A massive red flag. Trying to claim divine-adjacent power for themselves puts the Synod on high alert, though they might hold off immediate action to watch carefully first.
        
    - _Clock impact:_ **+3–4 ticks.** Enough to move them into the “active concern” range but still short of all-out manhunt unless compounded by further sins.
