Description
	The phial is warm to the touch, despite its age. When uncorked, the honey inside glistens with an unnatural depth -- reddish-black, like sap mixed with shadow. A faint floral scent lingers unnaturally long in the air
Vision (if tasted or used in a ritual)
	The fream is brief and haunting. The drinker finds themselves in a vast, candlelit hall with nine empty thrones arranged in a circle -- silent, dust-covered, and surrounded by offerings that rot untouched. High above, the stars are missing from the ceiling - like eyes gouged out of heaven.
	In the center, a golden mask lies cracked on the floor, leaking honey as if bleeding

Adventure hook ideas:
	Scholars may recognize the layout of the hall as matching forbidden iconography: The Circles Below.
	A surviving priest or oracle could interpret the honey as part of a funerary rite that was never completed
	Players may later encounter a godless cult still performing rituals with this blackened honey, trying to 'feed' the vanished gods back into existence.
