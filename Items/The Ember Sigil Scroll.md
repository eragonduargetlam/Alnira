A scorched vellum scroll, wrapped in black linen. Its seal is broken, but the was bears the impression of a spiral of ash curling inward -- a symbol unfamiliar to most, but faintly reminiscent of a long-lost warding mark.

The scroll contains a verse written in an archaic dialect. Roughly translated it reads:
	When fire fails and sky forgets
	When Circle hollows deep regret
	The ash shall stir in silent breath--
	The balance breaks, the Wardens wake

Careful study reveals margins filled with annotations from a long-dead scholar -- rambling thoughts about [[the guardians of liminality]], [[the concord's erasure]], and a recurring phrase: "Ash does not burn. Ash remembers."
