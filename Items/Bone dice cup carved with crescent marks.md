Description:
	Smooth and cold, the dice cup is carved from ancient bone, the size suggesting a giant-kin or perhaps something stranger. Around its rim spiral nine crescent moons, each subtly different - suggesting a cycle, or perhaps a countdown. The interior smells faintly of myrrh and ash. No dice are present, yet when shaken, it faintly rattles - as though remembering a roll long past.

Function:
	When used during a ritual of chance (casting of bones, gambling ) the user sees a flicker of something else:
		A night sky without stars. A voice - distant, hollow - whispers "None remain to answer"
		The moons on the cup flare briefly in the vision, glowing like dying embers, and then are snuffed out one by one.

Adventure hooks:
	A mad seer or broken oracle might recognize the cup and weep upon seeing it, claiming they haven't "heard the moons speak" in years
	A dusty tome might describe the cup as one of the "Lesser Veins of Communion" - tools through which mortals once received divine fragments.
	Players could eventually seek out the other tools (mirros, harps, chalices) in hope of reopening communication to the vanished gods or what else might be in the realm.

Additional possible prophetic whisper:
	"The thread is cut. The loom still turns. The pattern frays."
