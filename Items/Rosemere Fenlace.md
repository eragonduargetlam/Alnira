	With crimson knots beyond compare.

	In secret patterns only Rosemere knows"

Rare and coveted silk originating from the secluded village of [[Rosemere]]

Strands are collected from the abandoned webs of the [[giant spider]]s native to the area after a blood rain. This rain confers unique properties to the silk:
	Remarkably durable and easy to clean
	Not natively enchanted, but the threads accept enchantment readily
	Each thread features naturally complex knots as a result of originally being woven into webs
	These knots allow skilled artisans to draw out intricate, unique patterns

The [[Threadwarden's Guild]] works the raw silk into bolts of cloth. 
Every bolt of Fenlace is unique, owing to the natural structure of the silk and the personal signature of the weaver.

Origin is tightly controlled secret

A product for the wealthiest nobles, garments are usually passed down the generations as heirlooms. 

Merchants typically need guild favor to trade in this silk in the open, though occasionally it can be found through black market connections.

A bolt was stolen and used as a ritual runner in [[Beneath the Festival Moon (completed 8.31.25)]]
