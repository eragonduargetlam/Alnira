## 🜃 **The Red Archive**

_Subterranean annex of the Arcanostratum — “Where knowledge burns, we measure the ash.”_

---
### **Overview**

- **Access Level:** Restricted to Archmagus-General, two senior Strategoi, and the Keeper of Records.    
- **Location:** Sealed below Halst-Vaen, accessed through a staircase without shadow. The stair is said to exist only when unobserved directly.    
- **Purpose:** Containment and analysis of dangerous magical doctrines from the Malelic era and earlier.    
- **Public Cover:** Officially, the Red Archive is an “experimental warding vault.” Cadets refer to it only as “the Deep Hall.”    

---
### **Atmosphere & Aesthetic**

- Air smells of burnt salt and parchment.    
- Warding sigils crawl and rearrange like living script.    
- No torches — light seeps from the stone itself in pulses matching a human pulse.    
- Whispering echoes return answers to questions not asked aloud.    

---
### **Known Contents (Confirmed & Rumored)**

|Category|Description|Risk Level|
|---|---|---|
|**Malelic Codices**|Fragmented manuals of “Command Geometry” — rituals to impose will upon mass consciousness. Most unreadable without inducing nausea or vertigo.|⚠️ High|
|**The Glass Menagerie**|Crystal jars containing frozen echoes of failed summoning attempts; the sound of breaking glass sometimes precedes a ward breach.|⚠️ Moderate|
|**The Spine Array**|A sequence of carved vertebrae, each humming a different harmonic; believed to store portions of an ancient sentient ward.|⚠️ Critical|
|**Project Resonant Field**|Attempts to weaponize divine silence into anti-theurgic null zones. Research terminated “without conclusion.”|⚠️ Critical|
|**Codex of Unfinished Equilibria**|A document allegedly penned by Malel himself, exploring how to stabilize divine and mortal energies through sacrifice ratios. The final page is missing.|⚠️ Unknown|
