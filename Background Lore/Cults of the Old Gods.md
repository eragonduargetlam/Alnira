[[The Emberroot Circle]]
Deep beneath a grove of black-trunked ash trees, druids of the Emberroot Circle preserve whispered rites of the [[Old Flame]]. They serve as wardens of old thresholds and speak of fire not as destruction, but remembrance

[[The Ninth Vein]]
Once a cloister of visionaries obsessed with divine pressure points in the mortal world, the Ninth Vein believed [[The Circles Below]] would bleed again when the heavens grew quiet. With the recent [[Ruptures]], their ancient prophecies stir anew, and long-abandoned sites once used for esoteric alignments now show troubling signs of activity.
