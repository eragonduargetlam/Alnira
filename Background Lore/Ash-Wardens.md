Children's Rhyme
	Ash-Wardens wake when the embers die,
	Cloaked in smoke, they pass you by.
	They don't knock, and they don't stay--
	Just keep the dark and cold at bay.
	So hush your voice and mind your flame--
	They guard us still, but not for fame.

Before [[Dawn Concord]], when the [[Old Gods]] still held sway, the Ash-Wardens were stewards of sacred balance. Neither clerics nor warriors in the usual sense, they were guardians of thresholds --between realms, between gods, between what was and what may come. They kept watch when others forgot to, tending ancient flame-altars and marking celestial drift with grave solemnity.

They knew that the [[Hallows Above]] and [[The Circles Below]] must remain in tension, neither ascending nor collapsing. When the gods of ambition began their climb -- those that would become the [[New Gods]] -- the Ash-Wardens warned against it. When [[Malel]] rose, wielding truth like a blade and cruelty like a banner, they stood in his way.

But they were few, and he had many.

Their order did not survive Malel's rise. Their pyres burned cold, their texts scattered or forbidden. Only whispers remain now- chalked in children's hands, hummed in lullabies, or echoing in half-forgotten corners of the world.
