# 🜓 **Marradûn** — _“The Hands of Time”_

_A city that crafts eternity, one gesture at a time._

## ⚙️ Overview

- **Region:** Northern **Morvain** highlands, beneath basalt cliffs and bioluminescent fungal groves.    
- **Population:** ~18,000 (predominantly **elves**, **drakona**, **inferis**, **clank**, and **fungril**, with a scattering of mortals seeking mentorship).    
- **Government:** Decentralized **Guild-Council** of master artisans and clockwrights.    
- **Tone:** Quiet awe; light refracted through glass workshops, slow hammers echoing in cavernous forges, the city breathing in rhythm with unseen gears.    
- **Exports:** Irreplicable craftwork, heirlooms made by hand, instruments that age beautifully but cannot be copied.    
- **Imports:** Food, pigments, and raw materials — time, not trade, is Marradûn’s wealth.    
- **Common Saying:** _“A thing well-made is a life well-spent.”_
    

## 🪶 Cultural Core

### ✦ Time as Currency

- Every masterpiece records the **years given to it** — etched, counted, and honored.    
- An object’s worth is measured in devotion, not brilliance. A blade forged over four decades is more revered than one conjured perfectly in a day.    
- Marradûn’s archivists maintain _ledgers of time_ — a civic record of what each citizen has chosen to spend their years upon.
    
### ✦ The Sanctity of Handcraft

- Magic is a _tool for living_, not for creating. It cleans, builds, and preserves — but **never replaces the hand**.    
- To use magic to quicken a craft is to cheat oneself of experience.    
- “Magic saves time,” they say, “but only the patient earn it.”
    
### ✦ Eternity in Motion

- Long-lived citizens treat the passage of time as sacred raw material.    
- Every tower, bridge, and statue appears partially unfinished — scaffolds left in place, not from neglect, but reverence for _continuation.    
- To mortals, the city feels beautiful but melancholy — as though perpetually holding its breath.
