#Location 
Wealthy trading city east of [[Lydborough]]
[[Bannick Halder]] intended to sell his [[Rosemere Fenlace]] here.
- **East of Lydborough**, approximately **3 days on foot** via the old trade road.
    The route gently descends from **forested foothills** into **lowland marshes** before finally arriving at the **river delta** where Velcairn stands.    
- Terrain shifts from **pine and mossy stone** to **wet grasslands, peat flats**, and eventually the **tidal Shattertongue Delta** — red-tinged and silt-heavy.
### 1. **[[Saltpier Landing]]**
**Function**: Maritime access — docks, shipping, basic taverns, customs.  
**Class Range**: Low to mid.
- Heart of river and sea traffic.    
- Loud, briny, chaotic — first impression for most travelers.    
- Inns range from flop houses to modest but clean establishments.  
**Key Locations**:
- **The Saltpier** – Main mooring point, dock cranes, harbor offices.    
- **The Brinehouse** – Affordable bunkhouse for sailors and caravanners.    
- **[[The Hook & Rudder]]** – Mid-range tavern with live music and a backroom dice den.
---
### 2. **[[Stonemarket]]**
**Function**: Central mercantile district — goods exchange, crafts, regulated vendors.  
**Class Range**: Mid.
- Operates as Velcairn’s commercial heart.    
- Canals thread between stalls and shopfronts.    
- Travelers find equipment, supplies, maps, and gossip here.
**Key Locations**:
- **The Market Girdle** – Multi-tiered plaza for rotating trade stalls.    
- **Cooper’s Span** – Craft shops including smiths, cobblers, and scroll vendors.    
- **[[The Drafted Mug]]** – Popular mid-tier inn with decent food and clean beds.
- [[The Tallyman’s Vaults]] - [[Gilded Knives]] front and headquarters
---
### 3. **[[Murkbend]]**
**Function**: Laborer housing, unregulated trade, gray-market goods.  
**Class Range**: Low.
- Situated along a slow canal bend prone to fog and silt buildup.    
- Crowded tenements, shanty-built stalls, and informal trade spots.    
- Dangerous at night; cheap by necessity.
**Key Locations**:
- **[[Lowline Crossing]]** – Fenceline of hawkers selling reclaimed or stolen goods.    
- **[[The Pitchkettle]]** – A riverside dive with stiff drinks and unlicensed gambling.    
- **Trenchway Path** – Underused canal-side towpath; often watched.
---
### 4. **[[Gildwater Rise]]**
**Function**: Upper-class villas, foreign merchant houses, aspirational grandeur.  
**Class Range**: High (but visible from below).
- Built atop levees and stone terraces above floodline.    
- Walled gardens, carriage boulevards, and guard patrols.    
- Locals call it “the rise” — not unreachable, but closely watched.
**Key Locations**:
- **[[Petal and Thorn]]** – A famed, glass-roofed greenhouse estate with rare flora.    
- **Amber Row** – Street of estate-front specialty shops with exorbitant prices.    
- **Gildwater Gate** – Guarded stone arch with checkpoint into the district.
---
### 5. **[[Canalweft]]**
**Function**: Residential maze of townhouses, modest shrines, mid-class lodging.  
**Class Range**: Low-mid to mid-high.
- Crisscrossed with narrow canals and pedestrian bridges.    
- Home to scribes, minor officials, and merchant clerks.    
- Affordable lodging exists here for cautious travelers with coin.
**Key Locations**:
- **[[Weaver’s Rest]]** – Quiet, discreet inn favored by traveling scholars and smugglers.    
- **The Small Bell** – Public shrine to the River’s Mercy, tended by orphans.    
- **Runnel Court** – Apartment square with shaded colonnades and open washing basins.
---
### 6. **[[Farrier’s Hollow]]**
**Function**: Outer district with stables, caravansaries, land-trade hubs.  
**Class Range**: Low to mid.
- Sits just inland from Saltpier and beneath the rise.    
- Animal reek and caravan noise dominate the streets.    
- A fallback place to sleep or resupply for footsore travelers.
**Key Locations**:
- **Copperback Stables** – Services pack animals, carts, and river barges.    
- **The Hollow Hearth** – Big common-room inn with food for all classes.    
- **Smithy Circle** – Ring of blacksmiths, wheelwrights, and armorers.
---

## ⚓ **Velcairn: The Gilded City of Coin and Current**

### _“Tide favors the ready.”_

1. **“The hand that weighs the coin writes the law.”** – Money is sovereignty.
    
2. **“Even thieves pay the ferryman.”** – Acknowledgement of systems and tolls.
    
3. **“Never close a door that might sell for scrap.”** – Opportunism.
    
4. **“The clever drown slower.”** – Survivalist wit.
    
5. **“Truth’s price changes daily.”** – Relativism.
    
6. **“If it’s not in ink, it’s not real.”** – Bureaucratic cynicism.
    
7. **“Wind fills any sail; the wise trim often.”** – Adaptability.
    
8. **“Gossip is free until it isn’t.”** – Information as currency.
    
9. **“Better a full purse than a clean conscience.”** – Self-aware corruption.
    
10. **“A smiling liar sells twice.”** – Salesmanship as virtue.
