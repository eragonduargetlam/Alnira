**Berynspire**  
Northern city famed for ancient observatories and scholars studying the [[Old Gods]]. Gateway to the [[Ninefold Stair ruins]].

**Description:** Hill-perched city of learning and exile, once the site of an Ash-Warden tower. Now a haven for scholars, spellwrights, and ex-theologians.  
**Vibe:** Forgotten libraries, experimental magic, arcane fractures, and cult spies.
