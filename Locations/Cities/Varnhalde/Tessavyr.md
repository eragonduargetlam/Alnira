# 🜏 Tessavyr, the Bound City

_“Better a light contained than a flame unbridled.”_

---
## ⚙️ Overview

- **Region:** Outer cliffs of the Shattertongue Delta, southwest of [[Velcairn]]    
- **Population:** ~22,000 (⅕ licensed arcanists, ⅖ labor caste, remainder tradefolk)    
- **Government:** Civic council under the _Arcanum Charter_ — non-theocratic, “neutral” in faith and faction    
- **Tone:** Orderly, anxious, bright stone facades veiling exhaustion; humming containment pylons everywhere    
- **Exports:** Stabilized enchantments, glass conduits, wardstone, trained “custodial mages”    
- **Imports:** Food from Velcairn, desert glass and mana salts from the Seravaini trade road    

---
## 🩸 Districts & Landmarks

### ✦ The Veinhold

- Central containment spire built over the ruptured ley-wound.    
- Low, constant vibration — the city’s heartbeat.    
- Custodial Order controls access; some cells hold the _Bound_ (humans serving as living regulators).    

### ✦ Charter Plaza

- Civic hub, home to the **[[Council of Restraints]]** and public registries.    
- Magelights shift color to indicate curfew or magical surge levels.    
- Bureaucrats carry rune-stamped seals that double as minor suppression wards.    

### ✦ The Bindery Quarter

- Industrial slums turned containment workshops.    
- Rows of old rowhouses refitted with sigil-burned iron.    
- Black-market “unbinders” offer freedom at fatal risk.    

### ✦ The Glassways

- Elevated bridges of fused desert glass overlooking the sea.    
- Seravaini traders keep stalls here, selling mirror-glass charms believed to “reflect mana safely.”    
- Subtle clash of values — generosity vs. regulation.
    
### ✦ The Silent Shore

- Collapsed southern cliffs where the original disaster struck.    
- Pilgrims leave offerings of sealed candles to “honor restraint.”    
- Strong residual mana — minor, unpredictable wild magic zone.
