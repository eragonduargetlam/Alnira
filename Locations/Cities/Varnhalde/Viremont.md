**Viremont**  
The capital seat of power, located centrally in [[Varnhalde]]’s forested heartland. Seat of the ruling council, administrative hub, and home to the Ash-Warden Vaults

**Description:** A once-hallowed city, now a ceremonial capital built atop layers of Old God relics. Governed by the **Concordant Table**, a tenuous alliance of guilds, knightly orders, and trade families.  
**Vibe:** Marble facades, broken cathedrals, diplomatic backrooms, and ancient laws that no one dares enforce.
