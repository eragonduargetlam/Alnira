S**ecret Chamber – The Ashen Quarters**  
The stone gives way to a narrow passage, choked with soot and the faint smell of charcoal. At its end lies a hidden chamber, no larger than a cottage room, its walls cracked with smoke stains. The air feels stale, heavy, as if it hasn’t been disturbed in centuries.

Most of what once filled this space has been reduced to blackened heaps—cots, shelves, maybe even a writing desk—burned to cinders in some desperate act of erasure. Charcoal husks crunch underfoot, and ash stirs with every breath. The only things that survived are those too stubborn to be consumed: a warped iron brazier half-sunken in stone, shards of clay cups melted together, and the faint outline of a rune scorched into the back wall, half-lost in smoke damage.

Among the ruin, a fragile relic clings to existence: **a notebook fragment**, its edges singed but some lines still legible in crabbed, fevered script. The surviving passage reads:

"Some rituals succeed as they always have… yet others stagnate. I cannot tell if this is coincidence or misstep. Still, the pattern grows harder to ignore."

In the margin, half-burned and jagged:
"When the finger withdraws, where tip the scales?"
