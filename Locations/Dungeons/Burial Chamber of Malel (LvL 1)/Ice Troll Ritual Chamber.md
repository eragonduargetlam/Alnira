#DungeonRoom
Motivation: Warden/Sentinel of this coming King
	Fragmented speech, echoing visions
Read Aloud:
	The archway groans as you push the great doors open. Cold mist seeps across the stone, thick enough to obscure in some areas. Ahead lies a wide antechamber, its walls arched like a cathedrals' ribcage, black ice crusted over once-carved murals.
	Three crumbling pillars spiral upward, covered in frost and fading runes. One pillar lays in ruins. In the center of the room, a great Troll kneels before an altar with a crude idol surrounded by shattered crescent stones, its massive hands outstretched over frozen bones and warped metal.
	More bones litter the floor around the makeshift altar.
	In the corner, piles of ice appear to shift faintly sounding like glass grinding on itself.
	He lifts his head. Frost clings to matted fur. One eye is cloudy, the other glows faintly blue.
"He came. He... walked in frost. Or I did? Was I first? No. He burns with cold. You'll see it too. You'll see..."

Terrain and Features:
		Four Frost-Bound Pillars: 
			Block line of sight and may be used for cover. Each has 2 HP and can be shattered by melee or area attacks (automatic if hit with fire). Shattering sends a shard blast at nearest PC or NPC, requiring dodge challenge to avoid.
		Difficult Terrain:
			Icy floor: The center around the altar is slick and treacherous. Any fast movement (running, charging etc) requires a challenge roll (agility) 
		Divine Scar:
			The crescent stones on the altar crackle faintly -- the troll is using these to widen a rupture to see more of "the crown"	
Goblin Allies *if present*
		If recruited in room 2, they hesitate at the threshold.
			Trigger to flee:
				Ice troll regenerates to full HP after taking 2 or more HP in damage
				Two or more party members drop to 1 HP
				The troll utters: "You cannot see it, You are not first" on 4th turn
					When triggered, goblins panic. PCs may spend an action to attempt to calm them (DC 8) else they flee leaving behind makeshift flares (can halt regeneration for 1 round) or salt bombs (clear section of ice)
	Lair action: Hunger of the Hollow Flame
		Requires a PC to resolve a roll with fear
		Once per round *?* if any PC resolves a challenge with Fear, the Ice Troll's tie to the divine imbalance may lash out:
			"The frost deepens into an inky blackness, and the walls breathe outward for a heartbeat. You hear something behind your thoughts -- wanting."
		Effect: 
			A crescent rune ignites with icy-blue light. Choose one:
				A pillar bursts, removing cover and forcing a nearby PC to dodge
				The mist thickens, granting Ice Troll advantage on its next attack
				A whisper lashes a PC's mind, dealing 1 stress unless they pass a willpower challenge (target the echoing ward victim if any)
			If this action is triggered 3+ times, a dormant crescent stone flares -- may crack open to unleash a momentary divine echo. Narrate this as a foreshadowing of Room 5's consequences
	On PC victory: The Troll falls, its final words barely a whisper:
		"He Took it. He Needed it. *To fill the silence*..."
		The mist begins to fade. The crescent stones flicker once -- then die. A passage behind the central dais leads to a spiral stair, slick with frost and ash, descending into the final chamber.
