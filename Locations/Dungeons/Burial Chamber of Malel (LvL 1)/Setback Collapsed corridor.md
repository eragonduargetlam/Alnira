#DungeonRoom
Read aloud:
		You step into a long, slanted corridor. The floor here is uneven, buckled upward as though something below strained to break through. A collapsed archway ahead chokes the passage in rubble --not fresh. As you advance, a cold draft picks up and a couple stones are heard still settling somewhere within the rubble.
		On one side of the corridor, a half-toppled statue lies beneath a film of dark frost. On the other, a mossy stone relief has cracked open, seeping thin mist into the air. The air is heavy, frigid and a deep sense of rejection courses through you.
	Three Obstacle Pillars:
		Obstacle 1: The collapse (physical obstruction of rubble)
			The corridor is partially collapsed. Debris is unstable and seems to shift in subtle, unnatural pulses.
			Approach examples:
				Strength: Shift the largest blocks strategically
				Finesse/Knowledge: Create a support structure
				Divine/Elemental magic: Stabilize stone or reinforce with roots/ice
			Visual clue: 
			Failure consequence:
				Success with fear: minor cave-in but no significant impediment
				Failure with hope: mark 1 stress as you exert yourself to dive clear as the section you're working on collapses
				Failure with fear: Mark 1 hp and get pinned under rubble of collapse
		Obstacle 2: The Echoing Ward
			A chill mist flows from a cracked relief, whispering indistinct phrases. Anyone with a spellcasting trait may note this is not a trap, but a memory-stained imprint reacting to the presence of the party.
			Approach examples:
				Instinct/Presence: Focus through the echo, resisting spiritual confusion
				Presence: Engage the memory - speak a calming truth or a story of safety
				Knowledge: Attune to the residual energy to silence or redirect the imprint
				[[Whispered Name]] may get used here
			Whispers heard by those approaching:
				"He...must not wake..."
				"The lie was necessary. The seal must hold"
				"Too late too late too late too late"
			Consequences: 
				Success with fear: The mist halts, the next mental roll is made with disadvantage
				Failure with hope: Mark a stress as the whispers crawl through your skull
				Failure with fear: You stagger, your mind struck by a memory laced with an ancient fear like an icicle to the temple. Mark 1 hp
		Obstacle 3: The Twisted Passage
			A narrow section of corridor seems intact--but as players enter, spatial inconsistency warps their movements. Left feels like forward. Down feels wrong.
				Approach examples:
					Instinct: Track real orientation via dust, waterflow or moss
					Knowledge: drop items, test with ropes, check walls for consistency
					Team coordination: use paired movement to anchor perception (e.g mirrored steps)
				Consequences:
					Success with fear: You press through but the wrongness lingers the next agility or instinct roll is made with disadvantage
					Failure with hope: Mark a stress as you stumble up and left unexpectedly, dropping an item (have PC hand you their sheet and mark off a minor item)
					Failure with fear: Your mind reels as the disorientation overwhelms you. Mark 1 HP, disadvantage on next action roll of any kind
		Outcomes:
			3 successes: Corridor cleared, party advances cleanly
				A final echo from the relief - "You come with flame. May it not betray you"
			2 successes: Advance with complications -- no additional penalty beyond that of the above
			1 success: Partial collapse; difficult terrain added to Room 4
			For any complicated successes: The ward flickers and leaved behind a faint symbol - barely visible (DC10 instinct) a closed eye
			0 successes: Backtrack and reroute, providing time for Ice Troll to summon minions?
		Goblin Allies:
			If joined from Room 2
				Rubble: May use bone picks and leverage tools to help lift blocks (reduce difficulty by 1)
				Ward: One goblin hums - a disturbing lullaby that calms the mist briefly (advantage on the check for 1 attempt)
				Passage: They string up blue cord between the walls ("It's as simple as Blind Bones!") providing advantage to the first check of each player
			If they aid passage and survive (need 2 or more successes) they will give one member a [[Carved fang (goblin made)]]
