#DungeonRoom
[[Goblin Raiders]]
		Description:
			The corridor opens into a fractured large chamber, half-buried in ice and collapsed stone. Frost creeps down broken walls like roots seeking warmth. The air here is colder than outside, a clinging, unnatural, creeping cold.
			Several goblins huddle around a small, green-burning brazier cobbled together from ancient iron and cracked clay. They do not appear to be on guard and are heard arguing:
				"He's watching things that ain't there" one says in a wet rasp
				"He makes snow *inside*... that ain't right" mutters another, clenching a talisman carved from bone
				"We lost Kril and Bonechewere to the flames, and he just.. walked in after," whispers a third, face pale beneath its grime.
				"He said the King will come again," growls the largest. "And we'll be the first to kneel-- or the first to freeze."
			**GM NOTE: These goblins have not truly accepted the Troll as leader, they've been caught in his wake. They're frightened, they are confused and they may listen.**
		Options:
			Straight combat - [[Alistair's Torch]] can be looted. In this case, the combat does NOT trigger summoning in room 4
			Diplomatic approach (Presence 10):
				Success with hope: 
					The goblins don't see you as a threat. If treated with dignity, they'll talk openly. One may even offer guidance deeper into the tomb, or reveal the troll has started *speaking in dead tongues*
				Success with fear:
					They talk -- but they're deeply suspicious. One goblin warns the others that mortals "bring the fire again." They may follow, but only to watch
				Failure with hope:
					The goblins don't trust you, but one looks uneasy. Later, in another chamber, this one might break from the others to secretly aid the party -- or leave a warning.
				Failure with fear:
					The goblins pretend to go along with the party but they're lying. They'll alert the troll or sabotage progress
			Intimidation or violence (Strength 15)
				Success with hope:
					The goblins scatter, and one drops a satchel with blue cord and a simple ruleset for Blind Bones, a simple game in which players must hold this blue cord, attached to wall, floor or ceiling, in order to move (may help with Room 3 Twisted corridor)
				Success with Fear:
					The goblins retreat, but may wait in ambush at a later time
				Failure with hope:
					You drive them away but too late to stop one from triggering a rune-trap deeper in the tomb **GM NOTE use this to foreshadow the collapse in room 3**
				Failure with fear:
					They resist. A chaotic skirmish erupts. The noise is loud enough to trigger the Ice Troll to summon minions in room 4
