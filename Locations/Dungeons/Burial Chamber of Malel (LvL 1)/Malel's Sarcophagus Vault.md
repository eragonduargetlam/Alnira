#DungeonRoom
Description: 
		The vaulted chamber opens with a hush, dust hanging heavy in the still air. At its center stands an ornate stone sarcophagus, etched with forgotten runes and once-sealed by powerful magics. Now the lid stand open and intact, all hint of the seal erased as though it never existed. No remains rest within -- not bone, not ash. Just silence.
	Features:
		Clawed and booted tracks near sarcophagus
			Track or inspect Instinct or Knowledge 10, remind players with useful experiences
			Description:
				"Around the sarcophagus the dust beast strange, clawed prints trailing back and forth along the length of the sarcophagus. Fainter still, and nearly missed, are booted footprints. These do not belong to goblins or trolls, they are precise, purposeful and retreat back toward the entrance"
			Success with hope yields:
				You determine the booted tracks came first, and the clawed prints pursued. This appears to have been a mortal ritual, interrupted or answered by something inhuman. Whatever happend here, it was momentous -- and already over
			Success with fear:
				You determine the booted tracks came first, but the clawed prints don't appear to hurry, they seem almost reverent, worshipful. Something monstrous may now seek Malel in awe.
			Failure with hope:
				The tracks are confusing but one thing is clear: something fled this place that wasn't here before. A faint voice whispers your name once, before the chamber falls silent once more
			Failure with fear:
				The tracks are confusing but it appears that the booted tracks came first and became the clawed tracks
		Effigy in corner
			Knowledge or Intuition 10
			Description:
				"In a shadowed corner of the chamber, half-hidden under fallen rubble, lies a charred, half-melted effigy. It's a crude likeness of a man crowned in flame **GM note: Nearly identical to effigy in Lydborough, mention if players do not make the connection**. It's lack of dust accumulation marks it as a recent addition and the scorch marks around it form clean, perfect circles, not the chaotic remnants of an open, natural fire"
			Success with hope:
				You recognize this as the signature of a divine action -- A [[New Gods]] sacrificed something immense to intervene. This wasn't destruction - it was containment or banishment. **GM note: Consider binding a demon to Malel or having Malel's abilities bound**
			Success with fear:
				Same as above but an awareness washes over the PC: divine containment may not last forever. The flames here bought time but something has awoken
			Failure with hope:
				The effigy still glows faintly with power. Someone tried to perform the rite early, perhaps someone in the village knows more
			Failure with fear:
				This is a mirrored ritual, though poorly executed. Someone tried to bring Malel back or become him but something intervened at great cost
