#DungeonRoom The chamber is cool and dim, hewn into the bedrock. At its center rises a stone brazier, its lip carved in the shape of open palms, currently holding a faintly flickering divine flame -- pale gold with coiling wisps of gray ash rising into the still air.

GM note: This is the Trial of Passage, an instinctual reawakening of an Ash-Warden rite by the ice troll's crude mimicry, now held in place by the residue of divine memory

Environment and clues:
	A mural in low relief spans the eastern wall (Knowledge 10) It reveals
		A cloaked figure casting a ring into a flame while others look on
		Another figure kneeling with hands raised, interpreting glyphs on the wall behind the flame
		Above them both is a third image: two figures, side by side, each touching the flame with one hand. A circle of ash and gold surrounds them
	Below the mural, etched in Old Tongue (translatable with magic or intellect)
		"One gives what was theirs. One hears what was lost. Only together do the flames know peace."
	The brazier emits a warmth that shifts in tone as the party approaches: aggressive if approached hastily, calm if approached with intention or hesitation.

**Puzzle Hook**: The players must find a way to stabilize the flame to safely pass through a sealed archway behind the brazier. The flame reacts to intention, offering and insight -- this is a sacred test of sacrifice and guidance.

Player actions: Characters may attempt one or more of the following (or propose unique equivalents). Ensure actions embody sacrifice or guidance.
	Examples of sacrifice
		Cast a cherished item or keepsake into the flame
		Take 1 stress to "fuel" the flame with a piece of themselves (emotionally or magically)
		Spend a hope
	Examples of guidance
		Read and interpret the mural correctly aloud.
		Use a spell to understand the flame's memory
		Recite a prayer or invent a chant (perhaps use the ritual chant from the effigy burning) to reawaken the Ash-Warden rite.
			
Difficulty 9: Triggered once players engage with the flame directly. This is a shared scene, but each player who takes a primary action rolls
	Success with hope: The flame blooms with golden ash, recognizing the ritual. Passage opens and the character gains a boon
	Success with fear: The flame roils and lashes out, but the character endures. Passage opens, but the character gains a scar.
	Failure with hope: The flame resists--passage remains closed. The character marks 1 stress
	Failure with fear: The flame recoils and strikes the intruder. The character marks 1hp

Outcomes and Reward:
	Passage through sacrifice alone: The flame accepts the offering, but remains wild and sorrowful. The archway opens with a low, pained groan.
		Reward: Each participant gains an [[Ember Mark]] -- once, a player may absorb a magical effect intended for another, redirecting it to themselves (no damage mitigation allowed)
	Passage through guidance alone: The flame responds hesitantly to the ritual, confused but swayed. The archway opens slowly, as though remembering its purpose.
		Reward: Each participant gains a [[Whispered Name]] -- once they may ask a spirit, magical entity, or relic one question, even if it doesn't normally speak.
	Balanced passage: The flame burns clear and still. The chamber fills with golden light as both giving and knowing align. The stone doors open in serene silence.
		Reward: Receive the [[Ashen Knot]]: A one-time boon allowing the bearer to **stabilize a chaotic magical effect**, **dispel imbalance**, or **speak with a spirit who recognizes them as "whole."
		The Knot is a loop of braided ash-thread, warm to the touch. It dissolved upon use, leaving a circle of soot on the user's palm for one day.
