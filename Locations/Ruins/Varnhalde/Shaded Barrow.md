Used in [[Beneath the Festival Moon (completed 8.31.25)]]

Little more than a rise in the earth, choked with weeds and half-lost to time. A ring of broken stones juts from the mound like shattered bones, and at its center, the crumbled base of a ruined keep leans inward, its walls barely waist high now.

A broken archway yawns open at the center, partially swallowed by earth. Charred goblin corpses litter the barrow's entrance, twisted in agony, their weapons melted and scattered among scorched earth. Faintly glowing runes pulse weakly on the stones, the last remnants of a powerful ward now broken. From the darkness beneath, faint voices echo upward --hoarse, panicked murmurs laced with pain and fear. The words are hard to make out, but the tone is unmistakable: something has gone terribly wrong below.
