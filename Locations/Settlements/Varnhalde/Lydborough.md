#Location Introduced and central location of [[Beneath the Festival Moon (completed 8.31.25)]]

G - Government
	Council of elders - Primarily ceremonial, guide decisions during times of peace and festival. While the council maintains tradition, true authority is often deferred to the [[Flamekeeper]], who serves as both religious leader and community mediator. Practical matters are handled communally, especially during harsh winters.
		Standout: Flamekeeper [[Argenna Hollowbind]]
U - Underworld
	No formal underworld but secrets thrive. While there's no active thieves' guild or criminal enterprise, distrust of outsiders and hushed legacies of the past persist. Rumors swirl of hidden cult remnants or forbidden texts buried beneath the ruins, but such talk is quickly silenced. Children whisper rhymes about ancient protectors, and a few folk still leave offerings in strange places.
		Keep the [[Ash-Wardens]] in mind
A - Authority
	Community-first justice, guided by superstition. There are no true town guards. Instead, respected community members -like [[Yhessa Dunwheel]] - enforce tradition and mediate conflict. Superstition is often law; violations of festival rites or sacred customs draw swift, informal rebuke.
		Notable: Those who mock the festival rites are quietly shunned for a season
R - Religion
	The [[New Gods]] reign, but fear tempers faith. The people worship the [[New Gods]], especially those of hearth, harvest and protection. The [[Flamebound Exaltation]] is the village's holiest rite. Still, older beliefs persist in hushed tones - [[Thenya Mossroot]]'s blue sage bundles and murmured charms hint at deeper roots
D - Danger
	Encroaching Wilderness - Thick woods, tangled ruins and old-growth groves fringe the farmland. Wild beasts - wolves and twisted boars - were known to harry livestock. Some spoke of spirits in the woods: not malicious but watching. Hunters treat lightly near certain glades, and [[Thenya Mossroot]] refuses to forage under certain moons.
	The [[Divine Vacuum]] and [[Ruptures]] -As a result, the [[Shaded Barrow]] was breached. Cold winds and unnatural snowfall signal a shifting world. The [[Ice Troll Ritual Chamber]] and [[Goblin Raiders]] raid were symptoms - not causes. Deeper dangers lie beneath
S - Social
	Superstition binds the people. Folk are tight-knit and deeply rooted in ritual. See [[Flamebound Exaltation]]. Outsiders are welcome but watched closely.
