- **Lydborough → Velcairn Main Route**  
    ~3 days travel through Briarhold and Stillmere, crossing from forested foothills into swampy delta.
    
- **Rosemere Branch Route**  
    Sanctioned trade road branching west from Lydborough’s main road, winding through swamp and forest to Rosemere (about 1 day).
    
- **Velcairn → Skarrund Point Coastal Route**  
    Maritime and river shipping lane connecting Velcairn to the northern coastal city Skarrund Point.
    
- **Tharben Crossroads**  
    Overland routes radiating south and east from Tharben, linking to smaller settlements and mercenary trails.
