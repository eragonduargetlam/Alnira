- **[[Ninefold Stair]] (near [[Berynspire]])**  
    Ancient astronomical site, cracked and half-buried, draws pilgrims and scholars.
    
- **[[Malel’s Causeway]] (between [[Lydborough]] and [[Rosemere]])**  
    Fragmented ceremonial road from Malel’s era, shunned after dark.
    
- **[[Ash-Warden Vaults]] (beneath [[Viremont]])**  
    Sealed relic repository of the extinct Ash-Wardens, tightly guarded by the Synod.
    
- **[[The Broken Hallow]] (west of [[Rosemere]])**  
    Corrupted land scarred by [[The Gloamward Grove]]’s final ritual, devoid of life.
