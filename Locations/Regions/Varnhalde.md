#Region
Once called the **[[Erevain]]**, Varnhalde is a fractured and re-settled territory layered over ancient bones—of cities, gods, and kingdoms. From the haunted lowlands of [[Rosemere]] to the trade-throttled power of [[Velcairn]], it is a land still wrestling with what came before, and what is no longer speaking.

### **Geography & Notes**
- **Forested Foothills:** Surround Lydborough and Viremont; rich in timber and game.    
- **Swamp Delta:** Surrounding Velcairn, dense marshlands with hazardous terrain.    
- **Lakes & Rivers:** Central to trade and travel, especially near Stillmere and Velcairn.    
- **Ruins:** Scattered throughout the region, often dangerous but full of secrets.

## ⚔️ **Varnhalde — The Kingdom That Keeps the Watch**

### _“We hold so the rest may sleep.”_

1. **“Faith keeps its promises, even when we can’t.”**  
     → Duty as devotion, even when belief falters.
    
2. **“The gods see the hands that keep working.”**  
     → Labor as quiet worship.
    
3. **“We pray with shovels.”**  
     → Faith through action, not ritual.
    
4. **“If the dawn is late, we’ll light the first fire.”**  
     → Hopeful resilience under divine silence.
    
5. **“Every burden carried is a hymn.”**  
     → Endurance as sacred music.
    
6. **“We bleed so others don’t have to.”**  
     → Altruism without complaint.
    
7. **“A clean conscience weighs less than armor.”**  
     → Moral fortitude as relief.
    
8. **“The quiet believer still builds the wall.”**  
     → Faith expressed through persistence, not proclamation.
    
9. **“Dust and prayer fall the same on the faithful.”**  
     → Humility and equality before the gods.
    
10. **“The gods test the ones they trust.”**  
     → Suffering reframed as favor.
    
11. **“Good men still bury their dead facing east.”**  
     → Traditional gesture toward renewal and dawn.
    
12. **“If the light won’t come, keep the watch anyway.”**  
     → Obedience outlasting reward.
    
13. **“Kindness is armor thinner than steel but stronger than fear.”**  
     → Humanity as the truest defense.
    
14. **“We are the hands that lift the prayer.”**  
     → Ordinary people as divine instruments.
    
15. **“Every oath costs more than it says.”**  
     → Awareness of duty’s hidden weight.
    
16. **“When the bell tolls, work until it stops.”**  
     → Discipline as grace.
    
17. **“Doubt is not sin if you keep marching.”**  
     → Honest, human spirituality.
    
18. **“No one stands alone in the light.”**  
     → Communal faith, the soldier’s creed.
    
19. **“A scar remembers what faith forgot.”**  
     → Wounds as witnesses of perseverance.
    
20. **“Hope isn’t gentle, but it’s ours.”**  
     → Defiant tenderness; the backbone of Varnhalde identity.
