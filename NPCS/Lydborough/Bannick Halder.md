#Keywords Fretful, Sales-minded, grateful, opportunistic
#Lydborough 
Merchant visiting [[Lydborough]] for the [[Flamebound Exaltation]]
- **Heritage**: Human – sharp-eyed, travel-worn, dresses better than the locals    
- **Role**: Outsider vendor, opportunist, narrative foil to tradition    
- **Appearance**: Fine coat with dust-worn trim, flashy ring collection, cart with neatly arranged goods    
- **Demeanor**: Smooth-talking, self-assured, always weighing value—literal and figurative    
- **Core Trait**: _Shrewd_ – always working an angle    
- **Virtue**: _Charming_ – persuasive and socially nimble    
- **Vice**: _Greedy_ – profit over propriety  
#### 🎭 Roleplay Notes
- Voice: Pleasant and quick, with a merchant’s rhythm    
- Gestures: Constantly adjusting wares, brushing lint, calculating in silence    
- Phrases:    
    - “Culture is well and good, but coin is king.”        
    - “I deal in stories too—woven right into silk.”        
    - “Don’t be fooled by sentiment. It doesn’t sell in Velcairn.”
#### 🧠 Beliefs
- Every tradition has a price    
- Nothing is sacred if it’s in demand    
- He’s doing the village a favor by being here
#### 🧱 Mechanical Hooks
- **Haggle or Distract** (Charm) – talk circles around opposition    
- **Appraise or Deceive** (Cunning) – mislead or undervalue sacred items    
- **Elicit Secrets** (Charisma) – pry information while appearing friendly
#### 🔗 Relationships
- [[Argenna Hollowbind]]: Tense, she disdains his profit motives    
- [[Maeryn the Weaver]]: Eyeing her work for resale potential    
- Local villagers: Divided—some welcome his wares, others distrust him
#### 🎇 Festival Details
- Present for the **Flamebound Exaltation**, treating it as a “cultural sales event”    
- Holds a **bolt of Rosemere Fenlace**, intending to sell it in Velcairn
