#Lydborough
[[Flamekeeper]] of [[Lydborough]]
- **Heritage**: Galapa – textured shell, wise appearance    
- **Role**: Keeper of tradition, ritual leader, spiritual anchor of the festival    
- **Appearance**: Earthen robes singed at the edges, faint scent of smoke    
- **Demeanor**: Calm, slow-speaking, deliberate; rarely speaks without meaning    
- **Core Trait**: _Rooted_ – grounded, steady, immovable    
- **Virtue**: _Wise_ – speaks with insight    
- **Vice**: _Inflexible_ – tradition-bound, resistant to change 
#### 🎭 Roleplay Notes
- Voice: Low, deliberate, like smoldering coals    
- Gestures: Smells the air for omens, traces ash symbols when tense    
- Phrases:    
    - “All fires go out—unless we feed them.”        
    - “Do not rush the rite.”        
    - “The past is not a chain; it is a torch.”       
#### 🧠 Beliefs
- Ceremony is protection    
- Memory is sacred    
- Ritual must be earned   
#### 🧱 Mechanical Hooks
- **Perform Ritual** (Wisdom) – bolster wards, sacred rites    
- **Sense Imbalance** (Spirit) – detect divine unrest    
- **Deny Shortcut** (Command) – enforce ceremonial discipline
