#Lydborough #Keywords Superstitious, quiet, insightful, wary, fey-touched
- **Heritage**: Elf – slender, with sharp, knowing eyes and hair woven with small dried herbs    
- **Role**: [[Lydborough]]'s Healer, warding expert, secret-keeper of protective lore    
- **Appearance**: Earth-toned robes flecked with plant matter, hands stained with crushed roots and sap    
- **Demeanor**: Quiet, cryptic; speaks in half-truths and riddles    
- **Core Trait**: _Mysterious_ – keeps secrets even from friends    
- **Virtue**: _Protective_ – fiercely guards her community from unseen threats    
- **Vice**: _Elusive_ – never fully reveals her knowledge or intentions
#### 🎭 Roleplay Notes
- Voice: Soft, low, almost a whisper; calming but charged with subtle power    
- Gestures: Often clutches small bundles of herbs or charms, twirling them as she speaks    
- Phrases:    
    - “Not all hunger can be fed with food.”        
    - “The shadows dance where we forget to watch.”        
    - “Blessings are often warnings in disguise.”
#### 🧠 Beliefs
- True healing requires both body and spirit protection    
- Some evils must be kept outside, not confronted directly    
- Warding and charm-making are arts as vital as medicine
#### 🧱 Mechanical Hooks
- **Craft Protective Charms** (Spirit or Lore) – create wards against spirits or curses    
- **Herbal Remedies** (Craft or Lore) – heal wounds, poison, or disease    
- **Sense Supernatural Presence** (Insight or Spirit) – detect hidden dangers or restless spirits
#### 🔗 Relationships
- [[Argenna Hollowbind]]: Respects her ritual knowledge; shares occasional whispers on warding    
- [[Nym Garrel]]: Tolerates his superstitions; quietly supplies some of his ritual herbs    
- [[Old Breff]]: Occasionally exchanges stories and charms in private
#### 🎇 Festival Details
- Placed a dried bundle of blue sage at the festival boundary    
- Cryptic warning: **“To keep the hungers from circling.”**    
- Often seen at the edges of the gathering, watching and listening
