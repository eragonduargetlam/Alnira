#Lydborough #Keywords Pragmatic, Sharp, Cautious, Respected, Brisk
- **Heritage**: Firbolg – broad-shouldered, imposing presence; silver-streaked hair pulled back ightly    
- **Role**: Veteran market trader and long-serving [[Lydborough]] council member; known for blunt honesty and pragmatic leadership    
- **Appearance**: Practical, plain clothing with sturdy boots; usually seen carrying a ledger or simple scales for weighing goods    
- **Demeanor**: Direct, no-nonsense; cuts through pleasantries with sharp insight    
- **Core Trait**: _Unyielding_ – steadfast and firm, unafraid to challenge others    
- **Virtue**: _Pragmatic_ – focuses on what works, not what’s popular    
- **Vice**: _Guarded_ – reluctant to reveal personal fears or weaknesses
#### 🎭 Roleplay Notes
- Voice: Firm, clear, occasionally gruff; commands attention without raising volume    
- Gestures: Uses deliberate, minimal motions—points sharply, taps ledger when making a point  
- Phrases:    
    - “No time for fancy talk. What’s done is done.”        
    - “Light’s good, but fire’s worse if it gets out.”        
    - “The market remembers every failure.”
#### 🧠 Beliefs
- Tradition must bend to necessity; survival requires tough choices    
- Fear is a private matter—better locked away than spoken aloud    
- Leadership means carrying burdens quietly, not seeking praise
#### 🧱 Mechanical Hooks
- **Command Respect** (Presence) – influence the crowd or other NPCs through sheer force of will 
- **Market Savvy** (Lore or Craft) – access to rare goods or trade information    
- **Resourceful** (Resolve) – improvise solutions under pressure
#### 🔗 Relationships
- [[Argenna Hollowbind]]: Mutual respect but little warmth; Yhessa distrusts overly cautious ritualism    
- [[Bannick Halder]]: Experienced rival in trade; wary but professional    
- [[Nym Garrel]]: Frustrated by his stubbornness but understands his value
#### 🎇 Festival Details
- Keeps a single candle burning nightly—more superstition than ritual, a silent act of control    
- Candle’s flame can be used to relight the pyre in Act 3 if players falter    
- Stands apart from the crowd during festivities, observing more than participating
