#Lydborough #Keywords Meticulous, Observant, dry-witted, grandmotherly, proud
- **Heritage**: Firbolg – tall, quiet, eyes like sunlit moss; blends into surroundings more than she enters them    
- **Role**: [[Lydborough]] lorekeeper through tapestry; speaks to the past in thread and dye    
- **Appearance**: Wears layered robes of her own weaving, fingers stained deep with indigo and ochre    
- **Demeanor**: Soft-footed, observant, her silence speaks volumes    
- **Core Trait**: _Reflective_ – thinks in long arcs, not quick moments    
- **Virtue**: _Insightful_ – reads what others miss, especially emotion    
- **Vice**: _Opaque_ – speaks in metaphor, frustratingly indirect
#### 🎭 Roleplay Notes
- Voice: Gentle and resonant; when she speaks, it lands with weight    
- Gestures: Threads absentmindedly, even mid-conversation; touches others’ sleeves instead of pointing    
- Phrases:    
    - “Every thread is a choice.”        
    - “What we mend, we remember.”        
    - (Often silent, uses gesture or woven samples instead of direct answers)
#### 🧠 Beliefs
- Memory woven is memory preserved    
- Stories repeated without change are lies    
- True warnings come in metaphor
#### 🧱 Mechanical Hooks
- **Weave Symbolic Insight** (Spirit or Insight) – convey omens or emotional truths    
- **Crafting as Ritual** (Wisdom) – weave enchantments or bindings through sacred cloth    
- **Interpret Threads of Fate** (Lore) – detect patterns in prophecy or history

#### 🎇 Festival Details
- Tapestry Entry: **Lone figure holding a torch beside a crumbling wall**    
    - Interpretations: failed wardenship, fading vigilance, the last hope in decline        
    - May subtly warn of weakening divine protections in the village

- Known to gift pieces of tapestry to those she believes are about to shape a story
