#Lydborough #Keywords Earnest, Hopeful, Humble, Nervous
- **Heritage**: Halfling – wiry build, sun-flushed cheeks, hands calloused from labor and lute alike   
- **Role**: [[Lydborough]] born, heart of the people, self-taught singer with uncanny presence    
- **Appearance**: Simple homespun dress with a red sash, hair bound back in a scarf; carries a worn lute slung in a handwoven strap    
- **Demeanor**: Earnest, bashful off-stage, radiant when performing    
- **Core Trait**: _Passionate_ – sings with soul, not polish    
- **Virtue**: _Authentic_ – genuine to the core, never puts on airs    
- **Vice**: _Insecure_ – doubts her worth among trained performers
#### 🎭 Roleplay Notes
- Voice: Warm, powerful, startling in range when singing    
- Gestures: Fidgets with her sash when nervous; holds eye contact during a performance    
- Phrases:    
    - “I just sing what I feel.”        
    - “Some songs catch fire easier than others.”        
    - “A tune’s just a torch for the soul.”
#### 🧠 Beliefs
- Fire is more than flame—it’s memory, protection, and mourning    
- The Exaltation isn’t a show; it’s a promise    
- The old tales still burn if you sing them right
#### 🧱 Mechanical Hooks
- **Kindle Spirits with Song** (Heart or Spirit) – lift morale, push back fear    
- **Awaken Embers with Melody** (Charm) – possibly provoke magical or divine resonance    
- **Draw the Crowd’s Favor** (Presence) – shift festival dynamics with raw performance
#### 🔗 Relationships
- [[Argenna Hollowbind]]: Respects her deeply, treats her words like holy verses    
- [[Maeryn the Weaver]]: Feels watched—unsure if it’s approval or warning    
- [[Bannick Halder]]: Avoids him; thinks he’d sell her voice if he could

    - Chorus:  
        **“Burn him high, ’fore wind return—
