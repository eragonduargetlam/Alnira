#Lydborough #Keywords Theatrical, Curious, Fast-Talking, Grandiose, Secretly Insecure
- **Heritage**: Human – lean, weathered face, eager eyes, often distracted    
- **Role**: [[Lydborough]] born storyteller without formal training, self-taught and enthusiastic    
- **Appearance**: Plain, worn clothes; carries a small notebook for notes    
- **Demeanor**: Nervous enthusiasm, prone to overstatement but sincere    
- **Core Trait**: _Passionate_ – deeply invested in his stories despite limited skill    
- **Virtue**: _Determined_ – keeps trying to make his mark    
- **Vice**: _Overzealous_ – embellishes wildly without finesse
#### 🎭 Roleplay Notes
- Voice: Quick, sometimes stumbling over words; energetic but uneven    
- Gestures: Fidgets with notes or shifts weight nervously; occasionally animated when caught up in a moment    
- Phrases:    
    - “And lo! As Malel drank fire to know flame, so too did the flame learn to fear him!”        
    - “I may not be a bard, but these tales need telling!”        
    - “Every ember hides a story.”
#### 🧠 Beliefs
- Stories are important even if imperfectly told    
- Fame is earned through persistence, not talent alone    
- The Festival is a chance to prove himself
#### 🧱 Mechanical Hooks
- **Speak with Passion** (Presence or Charm) – inspire interest despite rough delivery    
- **Recall Local Legends** (Lore) – gather stories and rumors    
- **Motivate Self and Others** (Spirit) – bolster morale through enthusiasm
