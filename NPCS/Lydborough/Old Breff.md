#Lydborough#Keywords Irritable, nostalgic, conspiratorial, proud, stubborn
- **Heritage**: Elf – silver-streaked hair, weathered but steady hands, eyes sharp beneath heavy brows    
- **Role**: [[Lydborough]] elder, keeper of forgotten warnings and quiet truths    
- **Appearance**: Bent frame, stained leather apron always folded and carried, ashwood charm hanging from his coat pocket    
- **Demeanor**: Gruff, reserved; speaks slowly but with weight    
- **Core Trait**: _Cautious_ – wary of repeating past mistakes    
- **Virtue**: _Wise_ – remembers the hard lessons time tries to bury    
- **Vice**: _Pessimistic_ – expects the worst, prepared for it
#### 🎭 Roleplay Notes
- Voice: Gravelly, deliberate, often trailing off as if lost in memory    
- Gestures: Rubs the ashwood charm when uneasy or recalling something painful    
- Phrases:    
    - “The past don’t lie… but folks sure do.”        
    - “Barrels last longer than men, but both crack under pressure.”        
    - “Better a warning heeded than a lesson learned too late.”
#### 🧠 Beliefs
- History is a harsh teacher ignored by fools    
- Charms and rituals are last lines of defense against forgotten evils    
- Stories untold are dangers unheeded
#### 🧱 Mechanical Hooks
- **Recall Ancient Warnings** (Lore or Insight) – unearth old village secrets or dangers    
- **Steady Presence** (Resolve or Might) – calm others during crisis with his experience    
- **Invoke Protective Charm** (Spirit) – minor warding or luck boosts linked to ashwood
#### 🔗 Relationships
- [[Argenna Hollowbind]]: Respects her ritual discipline; occasionally seeks counsel    
- [[Nym Garrel]]: Finds his superstition tedious but necessary    
- [[Maeryn the Weaver]]: Believes her tapestries hold some truth—though she hides it too well
#### 🎇 Festival Details
- Keeps ashwood charm “just in case” near the fire ceremony    
- Observes quietly, rarely joining the crowd, but always watching for signs of trouble    
- Occasionally mutters warnings about repeating history
