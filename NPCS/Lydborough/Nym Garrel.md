#Lydborough #Keywords Blunt, loyal, weary, superstitious, private
- **Heritage**: Human – weathered skin, thick hands, permanent squint from field-glare    
- **Role**: Fieldworker and folk traditionalist in [[Lydborough]]; keeps to the old ways    
- **Appearance**: Wears the same faded festival cloak every year, patched in the same places; boots caked in old soil    
- **Demeanor**: Quiet, wary, observant; speaks only when sure it’s needed    
- **Core Trait**: _Wary_ – trusts patterns, not people    
- **Virtue**: _Steadfast_ – holds the line, never budges on belief    
- **Vice**: _Suspicious_ – assumes the worst from anything new or unusual
#### 🎭 Roleplay Notes
- Voice: Gravelly, low; mutters like he's always half-talking to someone who isn’t there    
- Gestures: Avoids eye contact, always scanning skies or flame    
- Phrases:    
    - “Some things you don’t prod.”        
    - “Wind don’t lie, it just whispers.”        
    - “If the fire looks back, don’t blink.”
#### 🧠 Beliefs
- The effigy holds power until it burns—touching it invites misfortune    
- Change in festival rituals is dangerous; tradition is what keeps spirits quiet    
- Flames remember—watching them shows respect
#### 🧱 Mechanical Hooks
- **Read the Weathered Signs** (Insight or Lore) – notices subtle omens others miss    
- **Stand Ground Against Fear** (Might or Resolve) – unshaken in supernatural presence    
- **Invoke Folk Superstition** (Spirit) – draws strength from ritual gestures or old sayings
#### 🔗 Relationships
- [[Argenna Hollowbind]]: Respects her only as long as she keeps to the old ways    
- [[Nella Raye]]: Fears her songs are waking things best left asleep    
- [[Maeryn the Weaver]]: Doesn’t speak to her—thinks her tapestries “invite things”
#### 🎇 Festival Details
- Refuses to touch the effigy under any circumstance    
- Known to stare unblinking at the flames during the burning, murmuring protections under his breath    
- Believes if **no one watches the fire**, it might **choose a watcher of its own**
