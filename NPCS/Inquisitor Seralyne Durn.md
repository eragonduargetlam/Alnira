**Heritage:** Human  
**Keywords:** Zealous, Unyielding, Coldly Compassionate  
**Role:** Enforcer of the Dawn Concord; hunter of heresy

- **Appearance:**    
    - Tall, broad-shouldered, late 30s.        
    - Wears layered plate, etched with the nine crescents of the Dawn Concord.        
    - Hair kept braided tight against her scalp, no vanity, only function.        
    - Eyes always searching—measuring people against doctrine.
        
- **Personality & Presence:**    
    - Speaks with calm certainty; tone rarely rises, but carries weight.        
    - Offers “mercy” only in the form of repentance and submission to the New Gods.        
    - Believes herself compassionate—saving souls from the corruption of heresy, even when her methods are cruel.        
    - Cannot be bribed, reasoned with, or shamed. Zealotry is her armor.
        
- **Equipment:**    
    - **Greatsword of the Dawn Concord**: blade base flares into a circle of interlocking crescents, hilt shaped like the concentric rings of the Concord’s emblem.        
    - Wears a Concord reliquary at her neck, containing a single shard of stone said to be from the Hallows Above.        
    - Carries writs of judgment, sealed with the Concord’s mark.
        
- **Methods:**    
    - Hunts heresy through intimidation, public trials, and swift executions.        
    - Values information from spies, informants, and common folk—stokes suspicion to keep communities self-policing.        
    - Believes torture is unnecessary; the guilty reveal themselves when faced with divine truth.  
    - Commands a small retinue of _Dawnwardens_ (armored followers who act as her hands).
        
- **Narrative Purpose:**
        - Serves as the _face_ of the Dawn Concord’s zeal.
            - Her suspicion of the party after Malel’s tomb ties them into the greater conflict with the Concord.        
    - Forces players to ask: _Do we align with the Concord for safety, resist and be branded heretics, or walk another path entirely?_        
    - Not “evil”—her cruelty comes from faith, not malice.
        
- **Arc & Escalation:**    
    - **Stage 1:** Suspects the PCs, investigates quietly, warns them they walk near heresy.        
    - **Stage 2:** Brings formal accusations, demands repentance or service under Concord authority.        
    - **Stage 3:** Becomes an active antagonist if the PCs resist—possibly leading the Dawn Concord’s forces against them.        
    - **Optional Twist:** Her faith can be shaken if shown undeniable proof of the New Gods’ corruption—or she may double down and become the Concord’s most dangerous champion.
