### 1. “The Quiet War Nears Its Resolution”

> **From the _The Dawnbearer Knights_**
> 
> Reports from the northern marches confirm that **peaceful settlement discussions are underway.** Field Captains have secured key positions and now turn toward _stabilization and relief._> 
> _Addendum (smaller print):_ “Regional militias are encouraged to provide temporary support to the stabilization effort. All volunteers will be compensated upon return.”> 
> **Between the lines:** “Settlement discussions” likely mean stalemates; the call for “temporary support” is thinly disguised conscription.

---
### 2. “Notice to Travelers – Road Safety Measures”

> **By order of Roadwarden Rennic Doul, Eastern March Command**
> 
> For your safety, **travel through Stillmere Crossing and the Old Timber Road is suspended** until marshway conditions improve. Unescorted passage invites risk from unregistered caravans and rogue scouts.
> Travelers are urged to follow the Velcairn Highway and comply with inspection checkpoints. 
> **Between the lines:** The “conditions” are likely military retreats or refugee movements being quietly contained.

---
### 3. “Guild Advisory to Merchants and Caravaneers”

> **The Velcairn Trade Consortium** issues a reminder that trade in **grain, iron, and lamp oil** remains under Concord regulation.
> To preserve fair distribution during ongoing recovery efforts, all merchants must present verified purchase seals at every checkpoint. 
> _A note in faded ink beneath:_ “Some seals seem to expire faster than others.” 
> **Between the lines:** Supply is collapsing and corruption is rampant — the “recovery efforts” mask a wartime economy on the edge.

---
### 4. “Letters and Offerings for the Front”

> **A public service of the Temple of Concordia**
> The faithful may leave letters, charms, and votive offerings for those serving in the northern marches. Couriers depart every fortnight at dawn. 
> _Handwritten in the margin:_ “Return materials may be delayed due to route conditions.” 
> **Between the lines:** Messages from the front have stopped. The silence is being managed, not explained.

---
### 5. “Administrative Positions Available in Velcairn”

> **The Office of Civil Harmony** invites literate citizens to assist in _record keeping and correspondence management_ for Concord authorities in Viremont, the capital. 
> Housing and rations provided. Discretion required. 
> _Someone’s quiet addition in charcoal:_ “More quills than swords these days.” 
> **Between the lines:** Paperwork has replaced victory; the bureaucracy expands while the war effort falters.

---
### 6. “Guidance from the Dawnbreaker Synod”

> **Issued under seal of the High Dawncaller’s Circle** 
> Travelers are reminded that **unsanctioned shrines, pagan rites, and roadside offerings** are dangerous remnants of a darker age. Such practices imperil the Concord’s light and invite spiritual imbalance. 
> Citizens are urged to **report suspicious gatherings or strange symbols** to the nearest Dawncaller or temple authority. 
> _A faint note scratched underneath:_ “Define strange.” 
> **Between the lines:** The Synod’s control is fraying; fear of the Old Gods’ return is seeping into everyday life.
