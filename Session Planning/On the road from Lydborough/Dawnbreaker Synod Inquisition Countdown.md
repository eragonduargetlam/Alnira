Think of it as 4 “ticks” that escalate the Synod’s posture toward the PCs:

### **Stage 1: Interest (default starting state)**

- Rumors in villages: “Inquisitors are coming.”    
- Locals mention Dawnbreakers passing through nearby settlements.    
- Merchants and priests are on edge, watching strangers more closely.    
- PCs feel _watched indirectly,_ but nobody’s naming them yet.    

### **Stage 2: Suspicion**

- Synod agents are said to be “asking after strangers.”    
- NPCs repeat eerily specific descriptions of the party.    
- Merchants and clergy hesitate to deal with the PCs openly.    
- Cultists grow paranoid, test PCs harder before offering trust.    
- PCs may find coded Synod markings (chalk sigils, warded doors) left behind.    

### **Stage 3: Pursuit**

- Evidence “mysteriously” goes missing or is planted against the PCs.    
- NPC allies warn them: “The Dawnbreakers know more than they’re saying.”    
- The road feels unsafe — rumors that the Synod “is only a day behind.”    
- Cult cells will scatter or deny knowledge if the PCs don’t use the honey shibboleth.    
- PCs may glimpse robed Synod envoys on the horizon (never close enough to interact yet).    

### **Stage 4: Confrontation (final stage, not before Velcairn)**

- Direct encounter: either an interrogation, a trial, or a raid on whoever harbors the PCs.    
- By this stage, everyone in Velcairn knows the PCs are _persons of interest._    
- Even neutral factions (merchants, priests) are pressured to hand them over.    
- If triggered early, it might be a **small strike team**; in Velcairn proper, it can escalate into the **Synod machine** in full force.

**GM Note:**
- Start them at Stage 1 after Lydborough.    
- Advance to next stage when:    
    - PCs dabble in _divine weirdness_ (ruins, shrines, cult contact).        
    - They _openly_ defy or attack guild/town authority.        
    - They spend too long in one place, drawing notice.        
- If they stay low-profile, the clock might stay slow — but it never rewinds.
