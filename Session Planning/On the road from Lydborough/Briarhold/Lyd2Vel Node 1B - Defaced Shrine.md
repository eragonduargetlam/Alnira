# Node: Defaced Shrine
tags: [guild, briarhold, shrine, divine]
funnel: Trade & Guild Tensions

## NPC
- **Acolyte Lethra (elf novice-priest)**
- Traits: nervous, idealistic, deferential
- Motive: protect shrine, avoid attracting Dawnbreaker scrutiny

## Scene
- Roadside shrine to the New Flame vandalized with guild sigils and charred offerings.
- Lethra implores PCs to investigate or restore the shrine.
- Villagers whisper the guild is sending warnings; some hint the Synod might intervene if the shrine is ignored.

## Stakes / Choices
- Clean, bless, or investigate the shrine → villagers’ trust shifts accordingly.
- Investigating could reveal guild influence or hidden messages in the vandalism.

## Clues
- **Sideways:** Guards at the [[Lyd2Vel Node 1C - Gatekeepers]] were uninterested in the defacement.  
- **Forward:** Rumors of caravans being harassed by the guild → [[Lyd2Vel Node 1E - Rival Caravan]] in Stillmere.  
- **Flavor Hook:** Graffiti reads: “TRADE IS FAITH,” faint ash smell still lingering. Could the guild be sending messages to insiders or outsiders?
