# Node: Hermit’s Cave
tags: [cult, briarhold, divine, old-gods]
funnel: Shadows & Secrets

## NPC
- **Old Sereth (firbolg hermit, Ash-Warden descendant)**
- Traits: cryptic, fearful, eccentric
- Motive: pass on hidden truths; alert PCs to dangers of the Dawnbreakers

## Scene
- Mossy cave outside Briarhold, chalked with crescent symbols.
- Warns: “The Dawnbreakers hunt both shadow and flame.”  
- Will point out the [[Sealed Clay Phial of Blackened Honey]] as a symbol of trust

## Stakes / Choices
- Win trust → gain potential shortcuts with cults later.  
- Offend or threaten → risk magical backlash or Sereth disappearing into the swamp.

## Clues
- **Sideways:** Mentions the crescent markings in ruins → [[Lyd2Vel Node 2B- Crescent Ruins]].  
- **Forward:** Talks about a gathering of hidden followers east → [[Lyd2Vel Node 2D - Hidden Cult Cell]] in Stillmere.  
- **Flavor Hook:** The chalk markings faintly shimmer in moonlight; his warnings hint that even the road itself is watched.
