# Node: Drowned Road
tags: [cult, briarhold, travel, hazard]
funnel: Shadows & Secrets

## NPC
- **The Ferryman (spectral presence)**
- Traits: cryptic, mournful
- Motive: warn travelers, preserve secrets

## Scene
- Road flooded; spectral ferryman appears, speaking in riddles: “Blood and flame follow the road.”  

## Stakes / Choices
- Solve riddles → safer passage or secret knowledge.  
- Ignore or act violently → risk getting lost or drawing unwanted attention.

## Clues
- **Sideways:** Flooded road aligns with [[Lyd2Vel Node 2B- Crescent Ruins]].  
- **Forward:** Whispers of a blighted caravan heading east → [[Lyd2Vel Node 2F - Poisoned Caravan]] in Stillmere.  
- **Flavor Hook:** Water black with algae, faint lantern glow in the fog; his voice drifts like wind through reeds.
