# Node: Traveling Merchant
tags: [guild, briarhold, hook, trade]
funnel: Trade & Guild Tensions

## NPC
- **Syrin T’Vahl (Katari merchant)**
- Traits: sly, opportunistic, alert
- Motive: move his cargo (lamp oil and fruit) safely to Stillmere; avoid Dawnbreaker attention

## Scene
- PCs encounter Syrin near Briarhold’s market, bemoaning Velcairn guild tariffs and worrying about inquisitors.
- He hints that he has a stash he needs to move discreetly, but can’t trust the roads or local guards.
- Lamp oil and fruit are his cover; what he really wants is safe passage to the Smuggler’s Inn in Stillmere.

## Stakes / Choices
- Help him transport goods → earns favor or potential access to secret trade knowledge.
- Refuse or interfere → he may become wary, secretive, or act to bypass the PCs entirely.
- Thieves or rival merchants may try to intercept him, giving the PCs a small combat or chase scenario.

## Clues
- **Sideways:** Mentions the [[Lyd2Vel Node 1B - Defaced Shrine]] and how the guild seems to influence even religious spaces.  
- **Forward:** Hints that his cargo is currently in a “quiet inn east of town, where things are handled differently” → [[Lyd2Vel Node 1D - Smuggler’s Inn]] in Stillmere.  
- **Flavor Hook:** Occasionally glances at passing travelers suspiciously, muttering: “The Synod’s eyes are sharp, but they miss a clever paw.”
