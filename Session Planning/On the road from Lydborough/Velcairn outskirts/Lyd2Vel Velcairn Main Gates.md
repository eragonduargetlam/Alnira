# Velcairn Main Gates
tags: [velcairn, city-entry, layer-4]
funnel: City Arrival / Story Hub

## NPC
- Gate Captain Arven Drost (human)  
- Traits: disciplined, observant, cautious  
- Motive: maintain city security, enforce guild and Synod regulations

## Scene
- Massive gates of Velcairn, bustling with merchants, guards, and travelers.  
- A checkpoint for traders and cult-leaning elements; city guards check cargo, question travelers, and monitor unusual behavior.  
- PCs can interact to gain entry, bribe, negotiate, or attempt stealthy infiltration.  
- Notices and rumors posted on boards hint at guild conflicts, cult activity, and missing shipments.  
- The city feels alive and tense — markets, docks, and streets provide rich roleplay opportunities.

## Clues / Hooks
- **Branch A (Trade & Guild Tensions):** Rumors of guild disputes over Rosemere Fenlace; potential jobs for the PCs in transport, investigation, or enforcement.  
- **Branch B (Shadows & Secrets):** Cult activity, espionage, or mystical relic delivery reported in the city; PCs may seek or be sought by Old Gods factions.  
- **Branch C (Story Hooks):** Requests for assistance from local authorities, guilds, or clandestine groups; opportunity to connect to Rosemere and other regions.

## Flavor Hook
- The smell of salt from the docks mixes with exotic spices from trade caravans; distant bell towers mark time as city life thrums with commerce and secrecy.
