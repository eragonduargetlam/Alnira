### Node: Abandoned Watchtower
tags: [trade, lookout, intrigue]
funnel: Trade & Guild Tensions

#### NPC
- Tower Watcher (retired soldier)  
- Traits: suspicious, talkative, helpful if treated politely  
- Motive: preserve the tower, earn coin or information

#### Scene
- Weathered stone tower overlooking a bend in the road. Old banners flap in wind.  
- PCs can climb to scout, eavesdrop on distant caravans, or find hidden notes left by prior smugglers.  
- Environmental hazards: loose stones, narrow stairs, gusty winds.

#### Clues
- **Sideways:** Scout notes link to the outlaw camp; tell where patrols are weakest → [[Lyd2Vel Node 1G - Outlaw Camp]]  
- **Forward:** Paths visible from the tower lead directly to Velcairn outskirts → [[Lyd2Vel Velcairn Main Gates]]  
- **Flavor Hook:** Faint markings on walls hint at past guild conflicts; broken arrow slits suggest recent skirmishes.
