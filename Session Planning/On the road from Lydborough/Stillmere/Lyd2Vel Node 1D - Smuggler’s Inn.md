- Traits: discreet, wry, cautious
- Motive: protect illicit trade, maintain inn’s reputation

## Scene
- Low-slung timber inn near the edge of Stillmere, nestled along the Old Trade Road. A faint smell of lamp oil and dried fruit permeates the air.  
- Inside, the common room is dimly lit, with sturdy tables bearing the marks of travelers who’ve lingered for days.  
- Syrin T’Vahl’s lamp oil and fruit are hidden in a locked storeroom behind barrels of ale. Varyss watches the room carefully, noting unfamiliar faces.  
- Patrons murmur about local politics, rival caravans, and strange travelers passing through. PCs can eavesdrop, negotiate for information, or offer help with the hidden cargo.  
- Interactions here can establish trust with Varyss or reveal subtle hints about guild tensions along the road.
