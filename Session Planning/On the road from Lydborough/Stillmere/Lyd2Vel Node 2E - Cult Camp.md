- Cultists whisper about Velcairn’s influence and the dangers of outsiders.  
- Small animal sacrifices, ritual symbols in the mud, and faint chanting fill the air.  
- PCs can gather intelligence, attempt to gain favor, or subtly disrupt activities.  
- Observant PCs may notice footprints or traces matching the Hidden Cult Cell, linking the layer together.
