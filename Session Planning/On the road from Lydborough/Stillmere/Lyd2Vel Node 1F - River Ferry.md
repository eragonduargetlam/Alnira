# Node: River Ferry
tags: [guild, stillmere, travel, hazard]
funnel: Trade & Guild Tensions

## NPC
- Ferry Operator Tamsin Rowe (human)
- Traits: talkative, cautious
- Motive: earn coin, avoid trouble

## Scene
- The ferry spans the swollen Stillmere River, timbers creaking under the weight of passengers and goods.  
- Tamsin warns of strong currents and recent minor floods. Small footnotes in her log describe previous cargo arrivals, including cryptic notes on “discreet” deliveries.  
- PCs may negotiate safe passage, assist in loading, or quietly observe the riverbank.  
- Local rumors include sightings of bandits along the bank or unusual lights in nearby woods. PCs who have visited the Inn might connect hints from Varyss’ ledger to these river routes.  
- Potential moments for tension: sudden gusts, minor slips, or curious travelers observing the party.
