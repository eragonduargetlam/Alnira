# Node: Rival Caravan
tags: [guild, stillmere, trade, road hazards]
funnel: Trade & Guild Tensions

## NPC
- Caravan Master Joric Dant (human)
- Traits: bitter, competitive, paranoid
- Motive: protect cargo, undercut Velcairn guild

## Scene
- PCs encounter a caravan slowed by a few scattered bandits harassing its path.  
- Joric is tense, gesturing to marks on the road and arguing with guards about possible guild interference.  
- Crates are marked with Velcairn insignia or coded symbols; some contain mundane supplies, others “sensitive” shipments.  
- Guards and travelers are nervous, offering opportunities for the PCs to assist with defense, negotiate for information, or discreetly investigate suspicious crates.  
- PCs may choose to shadow the caravan, learn travel hazards, or build a relationship with the wary master.
