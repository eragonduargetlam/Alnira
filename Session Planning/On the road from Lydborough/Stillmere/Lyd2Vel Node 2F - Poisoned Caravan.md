# Node: Poisoned Caravan
tags: [cult, stillmere, road hazard, intrigue]
funnel: Shadows & Secrets

## NPC
- Caravan guards (human, anxious)  
- Traits: pragmatic, wary  
- Motive: reach Velcairn safely, preserve cargo

## Scene
- PCs encounter a caravan showing signs of minor ritual tampering: discolored water barrels, herbs scattered among supplies.  
- Guards are jittery, debating whether the contamination is intentional or supernatural.  
- PCs may investigate, assist in securing cargo, or interrogate the guards for information.  
- Clues about nearby cult activity or the road ahead can be pieced together here.  
- The environment (forest edges, marsh smells) adds tension and subtle hints of supernatural interference.

## Clues
- **Sideways:** Guards mention encountering small cult activities recently, describing cryptic symbols seen along the road → [[Lyd2Vel Node 2D - Hidden Cult Cell]]  
- **Forward:** Reports of a secret envoy carrying mystical relics east → [[Lyd2Vel Node 2I - Secret Velcairn Envoy]] (Layer 3)  
- **Flavor Hook:** Herbs scattered in supplies, faint chanting; forest edges feel watched.
