# Node: Hidden Cult Cell
tags: [cult, stillmere, old-gods, secret]
funnel: Shadows & Secrets

## NPC
- Cultists (mixed heritages)
- Traits: cautious, secretive
- Motive: maintain secrecy, evade Synod

## Scene
- A narrow, dimly lit cellar beneath a derelict building; the air smells faintly of honey and herbs.  
- PCs see masked figures arranging small altars, candles casting crescents on the walls.  
- The cultists react cautiously to newcomers. Only those bearing a shibboleth (black honey token) gain trust.  
- PCs can attempt stealthy observation, social negotiation, or provide evidence of knowledge/loyalty.  
- Success may grant information about cult movements, secret paths, or hints about Layer 3 activity.
