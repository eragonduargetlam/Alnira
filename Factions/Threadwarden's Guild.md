### **The Threadwarden’s Guild** _(Artisan Guild)_ #Faction

**Tier:** 3  
**Description:** The Threadwarden’s Guild oversees the harvesting, weaving, and regulated trade of rare silks across the region—most notably the enigmatic [[Rosemere Fenlace]]. Beneath its official image of artisanal purity lies a vigilant order dedicated to preserving the mystery and monopoly of their craft. The Guild enforces oaths of silence and binds its master weavers through ritual and blood-price, ensuring no outsider learns the truth: Fenlace is born from cursed silk spun by the [[giant spider]]s and stained during the [[blood-rains]]. They are artisans, yes—but also quiet enforcers of their sacred trade.  

**Headquarters:** [[The Needlehall]] 

**Leader:** [[Deynna Halveth]] – High Threadwarden of the Guild, matriarchal and iron-willed, she hasn’t woven in years but still commands with the authority of one who remembers when the rain first turned red.  
**Known Agents:**
- [[Issor Wintlace]] – A quiet loomwright stationed in Rosemere, suspected of sensing the divine resonance in Fenlace but too loyal to speak it aloud.
    
- [[Kellin Roam]] – An envoy in Velcairn known for his silk-gloved diplomacy and brutal backroom deal enforcement.
    
- [[Talla Quist]] – Junior warden with an ear for gossip and a talent for weaving misdirection as well as cloth.  
    **Ally:** [[Velcairn Trade Consortium]] – Mutual protection and profit from controlled export channels keep both groups firmly aligned.  
    **Rival:** [[Gilded Knives]] – Persistent thieves and saboteurs whose recent interest in Fenlace poses an unacceptable threat to the Guild’s secrecy.  
    
    **Short-Term Goal:** Identify and eliminate the source of leaked Guild information in Lydborough before the Gilded Knives can act on it.  
    
    **Ultimate Goal:** Maintain total control over Fenlace production and trade while slowly expanding into elite magical textile enchantment—without ever revealing the silk’s true origin.
