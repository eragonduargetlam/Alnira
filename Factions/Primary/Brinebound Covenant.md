## Overview

**Type**: Criminal syndicate (smugglers, fences, agitators).

**Tier**: 2

**Activities**: Smuggling, extortion, bribery, sabotage, laundering, intimidation, assassinations, racketeering

**Goals**:
**Short-Term Goal:** 
	Hijack a Consortium warship undergoing repairs in [[Tarnroth]] and sail it under false flags to raid an offshore tax platform.  
    
**Ultimate Goal:** 
	Create a sovereign maritime free zone—a flotilla-state beholden to no crown or guild, where no trade route is taxed and no sailor sails under a master.
	Replace guild dominance with their own hidden empire.

## Story

The Brinebound Covenant is whispered about more than seen — a secretive criminal cartel moving goods and gold through Velcairn’s shadows. Most who claim to fight them have never laid eyes on a true Covenant agent. On the streets, their name is hidden behind the louder voices of the Stonehooker’s Assembly

## Philosophy

Break the guilds’ stranglehold; profit from the cracks in their power.

## Organization

**Headquarters**: _The [[Drowned Court]]_ — a hidden salt-crusted chamber beneath Velcairn’s flooded underdocks, lit by lanterns in glass floats.

**Leadership (named seats):**
- **[[“Saltfather” Iren Korrath]]:** Cold, deliberate mastermind of the Covenant; plays enemies against each other while keeping his own hands clean.    
- **[[Lysa Veyth (“Red Net”)]]:** Smuggling queen; rules the docks through bribery and loyalty.    
- **[[Bramlow Hask (“Coin-Loom”)]]:** Head of the Gilded Knives; velvet-gloved fence who launders stolen goods and bribes officials.    
- **[[Rorek Thane]]:** Voice of the Stonehooker’s Assembly; fiery labor agitator, more idealist than criminal, but dangerous in how he rouses mobs.    
- **[[Maiven “The Shrike” Eldrenn]]:** Assassin and enforcer; silent, terrifying, keeps the Covenant’s threats from being empty.

- **Public Mask:** [[Stonehookers’ Assembly]] — fiery labor movement fighting guild monopolies.    
    - Members rally around slogans of freedom and fair wages.        
    - Only the top agitators realize their “friends” are bankrolled by unseen hands.        
    - Most Stonehookers genuinely believe they are striking a blow for justice.
        
- **Militant Arm (external/public):** [[Stonehookers’ Assembly]]— riots, strikes, sabotage, fiery speeches.
    
- **Infiltration Arm (internal):** [[Gilded Knives]] — fences and fixers, laundering contraband and bribes.
    
- **Silent Arm (covert enforcement):** [[Shrikes]]— assassins answering only to Maiven Eldrenn.

### **Internal Rivalries & Alignments**

- **Saltfather Iren Korrath:** Absolute secrecy is his creed; keeps his lieutenants divided.    
- **Lysa Veyth vs. Bramlow Hask:** Clash between speed (smuggling) and subtlety (laundering).    
- **Rorek Thane (Stonehooker firebrand):** Believes in the cause, unaware of how deeply the Covenant bankrolls him; tolerated because he moves crowds.    
- **Maiven Eldrenn:** The Synod’s nightmare — keeps dissent within the Covenant quiet with a knife.    
- **Factional Balance:**    
    - _Cartel core:_ Korrath, Veyth, Hask, Eldrenn.        
    - _Unwitting mask:_ Rorek Thane and the Stonehooker’s Assembly.

## Resources

- Coastal smuggling routes and hidden caches.    
- Bribed dockmasters and harbor guards.    
- “Legitimate” fronts run by Gilded Knives.    
- Street power via Stonehooker riots (though not fully controlled).    
- Fear and silence — few dare to even admit the Covenant exists.

## Methods

Smuggling, extortion, bribery, sabotage, assassinations, intimidation, racketeering.
