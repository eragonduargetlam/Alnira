## Overview

**Type**: Religious hierarchy devoted to the [[New Gods]].

**Tier**: 3

**Activities**: Preaching, inquisitions, conversions, pilgrimages, excommunications, relic-hunting, temple-building, crusades

**Goals**: 
- **Short-term Goal:** 
	- Support the [[Concordant Table]]’s war efforts while spreading doctrine deeper into rural [[Varnhalde]].
	- Infiltrate known Weeping Sigil gatherings and provoke confessions of apostasy.
    
- **Long-term Mission:** Establish a regional theocracy where all Rupture sites are either sealed, consecrated, or erased from public memory..

## Story

The Dawnbearer Synod is the great religious power of Varnhalde, preaching the light of the New Gods from gilded temples. They wield the sword, the sermon, and the pyre to keep faith burning bright — and to snuff out heresy.

## Philosophy

Unity through faith, purity through vigilance, victory through divine mandate.

## Organization

**Headquarters**:  [[Spireshield Hold]] 

**Leader(s)**:  [[High Luminar Seraphine Vaelorr]] – A silver-tongued theologian who believes mercy weakens faith and ambiguity is heresy. 
- Charismatic, outwardly merciful, but ultimately pragmatic — the balance point of the Synod.    
- Sees the Synod’s unity as essential, willing to bend rules to preserve it.    
- Often plays mediator, but her “compromises” always strengthen her control.

**Prominent Members**: 
- [[Prelate Varan Tole]] – Head of the Dawnbearer Knights, renowned for his ruthless loyalty. Heading the war effort in the north
	- Blunt, militant, uncompromising; believes only holy war can prove the Synod’s righteousness.    
	- Distrusts Elyndra’s preaching as “soft” and sees inquisitors as meddling political agents.    
	- Allies most strongly with Mallien when heresy is involved, though their styles clash.
    
- [[Inquisitor-General Mallien Croke]] – Oversees inquisitions in rural territories; known for swift judgments.
	- Cold, feared, surgically efficient — sees heresy in every shadow.    
	- Relentless in his hunts; would purge a village to kill a single cultist.    
	- Tensions with Elyndra (too lenient) and Seraphine (too compromising).    
	- Privately scorns Varan as predictable, but respects his battlefield results.

	[[Inquisitor Seralyne Durn]] - Dispatched to Lydborough following the Flameboud Exaltation incident
    
- [[Voice-Marshal Elyndra Cauren]] - Commands the preaching arm; eloquent orator, beloved by common folk, secretly doubts the Synod’s excesses.
	- Eloquent, compassionate, deeply popular among commoners.    
	- Quietly troubled by Synod excesses — questions inquisitorial brutality.    
	- Strong rapport with rural folk, which Seraphine values as a counterbalance.    
	- Thinly veiled rivalry with Mallien; they undermine each other whenever possible.
- [[Chalice-Matron Hyrmine Kolvar]] _(Keeper of Rites & Relics)_
	- Traditionalist, ritual-obsessed, endlessly lectures on “purity of the clergy.”    
	- Distrusts all other arms of the Synod as too worldly or corrupt.    
	- Occasionally aligns with Elyndra in defending “the faithful,” but otherwise isolates herself.

- [[Acolyte Gera Veln]] – A rising firebrand cleric in Velcairn who preaches against “the grief cults.”  

- **Militant Arm (external):** _[[Dawnbearer Knights]]_ — holy warriors and templars, armored in gold-and-white, spearheading the war to the north.
    
- **Inquisitorial Arm (internal):** _[[Penitents of the Flame]]_ — secretive order of Inquisitors empowered to hunt cults, heretics, and sympathizers.
    
- **Preaching Arm (evangelical):** _[[Dawncallers]]_ — missionaries, sermonizers, and traveling priests who spread the Synod’s message to rural towns and borderlands.

### **Factional Balance**

- **Hardliners (zealous, uncompromising):** Jorn Thassel + Atheon Draemyr.
    
- **Softliners (merciful, popular, cautious):** Elyndra Cauren + Hyrmine Kolvar (sometimes).
    
- **Balancer:** High Luminar Seraphine Vaelorr — leans whichever way secures Synod dominance.
### **Tensions to Exploit at the Table**

- Elyndra vs. Atheon: preaching mercy vs. purging ruthlessly.    
- Jorn vs. Elyndra: holy war vs. winning hearts.    
- Hyrmine vs. everyone: ritual purity vs. political compromise.    
- Seraphine walking the tightrope: _“unity at all costs.”_

## 🔥 **Dawnbreaker Synod: The Voice of the New Gods**

### _“From shadow, clarity.”_

1. **“The light does not ask — it reveals.”**  
     → Faith as understanding, not imposition.
    
2. **“Every dawn begins with trust.”**  
     → Encourages belief before questioning.
    
3. **“Truth is a mirror polished by obedience.”**  
     → Order framed as enlightenment.
    
4. **“We serve so that others may see.”**  
     → The Synod’s self-image as guardians of revelation.
    
5. **“Even in darkness, the light waits for a keeper.”**  
     → Compassionate tone, but hierarchical implication.
    
6. **“Mercy walks hand in hand with duty.”**  
     → Soft justification for strict justice.
    
7. **“To question is human; to listen, divine.”**  
     → Deflects dissent politely.
    
8. **“Each candle is lit by another’s faith.”**  
     → Collective identity; interdependence through belief.
    
9. **“Let the dawn find no one sleeping.”**  
     → Call to vigilance — diligence as moral virtue.
    
10. **“The brightest hearts burn without smoke.”**  
     → Pure devotion leaves no corruption (and no trace).
    
11. **“The flame teaches by warmth and by scar.”**  
     → Faith includes consequence without malice.
    
12. **“Light shared is light doubled.”**  
     → The communal beauty of faith.
    
13. **“The gods lend their silence so we may answer.”**  
     → Explains divine absence as trust in mortals.
    
14. **“Discipline is devotion in motion.”**  
     → Devotion shown through structure and steadiness.
    
15. **“Forgiveness is the sunrise after duty is done.”**  
     → Compassion comes _after_ correction, not before.
    
16. **“To stand in the light is to cast no shadow.”**  
     → Moral perfection as the ideal citizen’s goal.
    
17. **“Hold fast; even the sun must rise by measure.”**  
     → Reassures patience and ritual consistency.
    
18. **“One truth, many hands.”**  
     → The Synod’s ecumenical face — unity through hierarchy.
    
19. **“Faith is the steady flame, not the spark.”**  
     → Constancy over fervor — disciplined devotion.
    
20. **“No heart is beyond the dawn.”**  
     → Hopeful, but implies conversion as salvation.

### **Synod ↔ Concordant Table Interactions**

- **Seraphine Vaelorr ↔ Lady Veyra Luthenne (Threadwardens):** Mutually respectful power brokers; both pragmatic, both know the war effort depends on Fenlace gold.
    
- [[Prelate Varan Tole]] ↔ Sir Corvell Drenn (White Ash):** Kindred martial spirits; align on honor and war, though Corvell worries about Synod zealotry.
    
- **Mallien Croke ↔ Tassir Montcairn (Bankers):** Cold alliance; inquisitors need funding, Montcairn profits from fear.
    
- **Elyndra Cauren ↔ Hessa Draemholt (Artisans):** Mutual sympathy for common folk; Elyndra’s sermons and Hessa’s gruff honesty resonate.
    
- **Hyrmine Kolvar ↔ Archon Relmar Dovreth (Nobility):** Both obsessed with tradition; Kolvar lends divine weight to Relmar’s aristocratic posturing.
    
- **Merrowind Faelcor (Consortium):** Distrusted by almost everyone in the Synod — seen as too mercantile, too slippery, not devout enough.
