### **The Weeping Sigil** _(Religious Order / Cult)_ #Faction

**Tier:** 2  
**Description:** A scattered faith movement mourning the silence of the Old Gods, who no longer answer prayer, dream, or omen. The Sigil teaches that the gods’ withdrawal is not punishment, but a test of loyalty and devotion. They now travel to regions where divine imbalance has manifested as _Ruptures_, believing these sites may allow communion to resume. Though considered heretical by mainstream churches, their rites attract those desperate for a return of the sacred.  

**Headquarters:** [[The Hollow Reliquary]] 

**Leader:** [[Father Corvayn]] – A quiet, fervent former cleric of a now-dormant god, whose eyes weep ink during moments of ecstatic grief.  

**Known Agents:**
- [[Ysala Vint]] – A wealthy patroness whose twin daughters died after the family chapel went mute.
    
- [[Goremund Rell]] – A scarred wanderer who speaks only in scripture fragments; he believes the Ruptures whisper back.
    
- [[Tassel Gorrin]] – A Lydborough folk-singer who subtly folds the Sigil’s beliefs into funerary ballads.  
    **Ally:** [[Circle of the Hollow Moon]] – A forgotten druidic thread whose grief rites unintentionally mirror the Sigil’s sacred sorrow.  
    **Rival:** [[Dawnbearer Synod]] – An orthodox faction of New God zealots who regard the Sigil as dangerous mystics.  
    **Short-Term Goal:** Identify and consecrate at least one Rupture site with a ritual of sacred lamentation.  
    **Ultimate Goal:** Reestablish contact with the Old Gods by forging a permanent sanctuary at a stable Rupture.
