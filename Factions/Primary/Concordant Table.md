## Overview

**Type**: Ruling council (guilds, knightly orders, trade dynasties)

**Tier**: 3

**Activities**: Tariffs, arbitration, monopolies, taxation, diplomacy, censorship, patronage, suppression

**Organization**: 

**Goals**: 
**Short-term Goal:** Secure alliances with the [[Dawnbearer Synod]] against [[Morvain]] to the north.

**Long-term Mission:** Maintain the Table’s stranglehold on power by balancing guild interests and suppressing uprisings.

## Story

The Concordant Table is the ruling council of Varnhalde, where the kingdom’s guilds, knightly orders, and wealthy trade families bargain, bicker, and scheme. From their seats in Viremont, they balance profit and power, always with an eye toward keeping the guild monopolies unbroken

## Philosophy

Stability through control of trade, wealth, and law.

## Organization

**Headquarters**: [[The Hall of Concord]] — a fortified marble-and-silk chamber at the heart of [[Viremont]], ringed with banners of guild crests.

**Leadership**:
- **Lady Veyra Luthenne** (_[[Threadwarden's Guild]]_): Stern, calculating, master of Fenlace trade; wields near-veto power.
		- Nearly untouchable due to the Fenlace monopoly.    
		- Publicly composed and practical, but ruthless in private dealings.    
		- Seen as overbearing by most other members, but indispensable.
	- **Likely Allies:**
		- **Tassir Montcairn (Banking Houses)** — relies on Fenlace exports to secure foreign credit and debts.    
		- **Merrowind Faelcor (Velcairn Trade Consortium)** — benefits directly from Threadwarden exports, though he resents her dominance.
	- **Likely Opponents:**
		- **Sir Corvell Drenn (Order of the White Ash)** — sees her profiteering as dishonorable, especially during war.    
		- **Archon Relmar Dovreth (Nobility)** — resents that “new money” guilds overshadow old aristocracy.
    
- **Sir Corvell Drenn** (_Order of the White Ash_): Knightly representative; martial, honor-bound, increasingly uneasy with guild dominance.
		- Martial voice of conscience on the Table, but politically isolated.    
		- Respects Hessa Draemholt’s honesty; distrusts merchants.    
		- Occasionally threatens to pull knightly levies from the Emberlance Host if the Table ignores military needs.
    
- **Merrowind Faelcor** (_Velcairn Trade Consortium_): Silver-tongued magnate; represents Velcairn’s markets, pragmatic and profit-driven.
		- Charming, urbane, always scheming.    
		- Needs Threadwarden silk but wants more autonomy for Velcairn.    
		- Frequently bargains with Tassir for favorable lending.
    
- **Tassir Montcairn** (_Banking Houses of Viremont_): Obsessive record-keeper; controls much of the kingdom’s debt.
		- Meticulous, paranoid, obsessed with ledgers.    
		- Almost always sides with the Threadwardens (money flows from Fenlace).    
		- Loathed by Draemholt for strangling artisans with debt.
    
- **Hessa Draemholt** (_League of Smiths and Artisans_): Gruff, practical voice for workshops and crafts; often bullied into alliances.
		- Gruff, practical, speaks for workers but gets drowned out by wealthier seats.    
		- Sometimes aligns with Sir Corvell on matters of fairness or honor.    
		- Secretly courts Stonehooker agitators for leverage — dangerous if exposed.
    
- **Archon Relmar Dovreth** (_Minor noble coalition_): Old-blood aristocrat; insists on preserving noble privilege against rising guild power.
		- Aristocratic, haughty, clings to old traditions of noble privilege.    
		- At odds with nearly everyone, but sometimes allies with Corvell and Draemholt against the guild bloc.    
		- Whispers with the Dawnbreaker Synod to strengthen the nobility’s prestige through divine sanction.

### **Factional Balance**

- **Guild Bloc (dominant):** Lady Veyra, Tassir Montcairn, Merrowind Faelcor.
    
- **Opposition Bloc (fractured):** Sir Corvell, Hessa Draemholt, Archon Relmar.
    
- **Swing Vote:** Draemholt (most likely to be bullied, bribed, or pressured).


**Dominant Force:** [[Threadwarden's Guild]](monopoly on Rosemere Fenlace).
    
**Regional Arm:** [[Velcairn Trade Consortium]] (direct control over Velcairn’s markets).

**Militant Arm:** [[Emberlance Host]] — a professional army of guild-funded knights and mercenaries leading the northern invasion.

## Resources

- Kingdom treasury (fed by Fenlace monopoly).
    
- Emberlance Host and knightly levies.
    
- Guild militias and mercenary contracts.
    
- Vast political patronage networks in cities.
    
- Monopoly control over Fenlace and trade routes.

## Methods

Tariffs, taxation, arbitration, diplomacy, censorship, suppression, patronage.
