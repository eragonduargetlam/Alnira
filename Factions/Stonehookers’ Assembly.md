## Overview

**Type**: Artisan Guild / Rebel-Aligned Labor Syndicate

**Tier**: 2

**Activities**: Sailing guild, syndicate funding the "freedom fighters" (funds seen as investment in future profits)

**Organization**: Masters, journeymen, apprentices

**Goals**: 
**Short-Term Goal:** Complete construction of a hidden slipway beneath Skarrund Point to refit and launch ships undetected by the Velcairn Trade Consortium.
    
**Ultimate Goal:** Establish an autonomous coastal shipwrights’ territory governed by Assembly law—where shipwrights answer only to tide, tool, and code.

**Player-Facing Hooks:**
- PCs might help secure or sabotage the slipway.    
- Join protests and marches, gaining trust of the common folk.    
- Discover hidden Covenant influence or mysterious shipments.    
- Legitimize the Assembly or expose its hidden ties, affecting future political sway.

## Story

The Stonehooker’s Assembly is a workers’ movement rising from the shipyards, quarries, and ropeworks of Velcairn. They call for fair wages, safe conditions, and freedom from the guilds’ chokehold. Their rallies are noisy, their strikes costly, and their leaders fiery in their defiance of the Concordant Table.

## Philosophy

Justice for laborers; liberation from guild tyranny; dignity in work.

## Relationships

**Ally:** [[Brinebound Covenant]] – While never acknowledged in meeting ledgers, they are the Assembly’s storm-handled hammer.  
    
**Rival:** [[Velcairn Trade Consortium]]– Their offer to “incorporate” the Consortium into the Assembly was met with silence, then sabotage.  

## Organization

**Headquarters**: [[Skarrund Point]]

**Leadership (public figures):**
- **[[Rorek Thane]]:** Quarryman-turned-orator; fiery, earnest, utterly convinced in the cause.
    
- **[[Jessa Cowlfen]]:** Dockside matron; practical organizer of strikes, known for looking after the families of arrested workers.
    
- **[[Tarren “Stonespine” Braye]]:** Veteran ropeworker; hulking, respected for his willingness to take blows on the picket line.
    
- _(Unseen Patronage: The Brinebound Covenant secretly funds their pamphlets, bribes sympathetic guards, and supplies weapons when needed.)_

**Internal Dynamics:**
- _True Believers:_ Rorek, Jessa, most rank-and-file workers.    
- _Hardliners:_ Tarren Braye and younger firebrands who want escalation.    
- _Hidden Hand:_ Brinebound Covenant controlling funds, planning, and arms.

## Resources

- Strong support among quarrymen, dockhands, and weavers.    
- Street-level loyalty in Velcairn’s poorer districts.    
- Networks of underground printshops producing pamphlets and broadsheets.    
- Local sympathizers in city guard ranks (bribed or genuinely sympathetic).    
- Unacknowledged financial lifeline from the Brinebound Covenant.

## Methods

Barter, bribery, extortion - when needed, wet work handled through subsidiaries
