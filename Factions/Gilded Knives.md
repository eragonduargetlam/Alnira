### **The Gilded Knives** _(Criminal Syndicate)_ #Faction

**Tier:** 2  
**Description:** More than common cutthroats, the Gilded Knives run smuggling routes, relic theft operations, and illicit auctions specializing in forbidden divine artifacts. They trade in secrets and sacrilege, fencing items plundered from sealed shrines or “purified” temples. Blending into both high society and the underbelly of cities like Velcairn, they now eye sacred trade monopolies as their next frontier. Few know what Rosemere Fenlace truly is, but the Knives want it anyway.  

**Headquarters:** [[The Tallyman’s Vaults]] 

**Leader:** [[“Mother” Kelrene]] – Proprietor of a luxury bathhouse in Velcairn where veiled whispers are bartered alongside scented oils; expert in brokering secrets and blackmail.  

**Known Agents:**
- [[Brint Calder]] – A relic-runner with a nose for unsealed tombs and an irreverent sense of humor.
    
- [[Maeryn the Weaver]] – A Lydborough textile merchant who launders sacred contraband in bolts of cloth.
    
- [[“Ratknife” Sol]] – Enforcer and addict whose knife dances are both art and execution.  
    **Ally:** [[The Veiled Lambs]] – A covert network that protects divine outcasts and sometimes shares intel or artifacts.  
    **Rival:** [[Threadwarden's Guild]] – Holds the secrets of Rosemere Fenlace and refuses all negotiation outside sanctioned channels.  
    
    **Short-Term Goal:** Embed an agent inside the [[Threadwarden’s Guild]] in [[Rosemere]] to steal techniques—or a weaver—linked to the creation of [[Rosemere Fenlace]].  
    
    **Ultimate Goal:** Corner the black-market trade in divine relics and rare textiles across three major cities, becoming indispensable to both cults and corrupt elites.
