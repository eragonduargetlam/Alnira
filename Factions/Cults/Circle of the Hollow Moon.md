- **Name:** Circle of the Hollow Moon
    
- **Table Description (player-facing):** _“A secretive cult venerating the vanished Old Gods, performing cryptic rituals in hidden groves.”_
    
- **Philosophy:** Reverence for the Old Gods through meditation, natural cycles, and symbolic offerings; patience over aggression.
    
- **Headquarters / Gathering Site:** _[[Moonfall Glade]]_ — secluded grove outside Velcairn, marked by standing stones and carved runes.
    
- **Leadership (named figures):**    
    - Alden Veymar — Elder mystic        
    - Sira Korrin — Keeper of the Runes        
    - Talin Brev — Novice mentor
        
- **Short-Term Goals:** Align the glade’s stones with the solstices; recruit a few discreet members.
    
- **Long-Term Mission:** Preserve and slowly reconstruct lost knowledge of the Old Gods; maintain sanctity of natural and ritual spaces.
    
- **Methods / Activities:** Moonlit meditations, ritual chanting/drumming, crafting charms, seasonal ceremonies, observing natural phenomena, recording visions/dreams, herbal/incense offerings.
    
- **Resources:** Hidden glade(s), small library of esoteric texts, simple ritual tools, trusted network of locals.
    
- **Player-Facing Hooks:** Offer guidance or mystical insight; need help securing ritual sites; missteps cause minor supernatural effects; potential source of Old God lore.
    
- **Internal Dynamics:** Elders guide novices; focus on collaboration and secrecy; no coercion or violence.
    
- **Relative Impact:** 1 (local)
    
- **Threat Level:** Low to moderate — hunted by Dawnbreakers, isolated, scattered.
