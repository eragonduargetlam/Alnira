- **Table Description:** _“A radical cult attempting extreme, symbolic rituals to pierce the silence of the vanished Old Gods. Their actions often border on danger to themselves and the environment.”_
    
- **Philosophy:** The Old Gods’ absence demands bold action; ritual power must be amplified to force contact, even at great cost.
    
- **Headquarters / Gathering Site:** _Bloodrock Cairn_ — a jagged coastal cliff site where ritual circles and sigils are carved into stone.
    
- **Leadership:**    
    - Varlen Karesh — charismatic, reckless, obsessed with divine communication        
    - Sorya Nahl — ritual coordinator and moral counterbalance, tries to keep followers from self-harm
        
- **Short-Term Goals:** Complete a high-risk, amplified ritual to pierce the divine veil.
    
- **Long-Term Mission:** Reestablish contact with the Old Gods and restore their presence in the world.
    
- **Methods / Activities:** Amplified chantings, fire and light rites, geomantic alignments, symbolic offerings, risk-laden experiments.
    
- **Resources:** Coastal cliffs, carved ritual stones, small dedicated cadre of followers.
    
- **Player-Facing Hooks:** PCs may be hired to protect or sabotage rituals, investigate mysterious phenomena, or negotiate with desperate followers.
    
- **Internal Dynamics:** Varlen drives followers toward dangerous extremes; Sorya restrains excess where possible; tension is constant.
    
- **Relative Impact:** 1 (local, high risk)
