- **Table Description:** _“A forest cult attempting to force small, experimental awakenings in the natural world to draw the Old Gods’ attention.”_
    
- **Philosophy:** Nature is a conduit to the vanished divine; small manipulations of plants, water, or stone may awaken latent forces.
    
- **Headquarters / Gathering Site:** _Gnarled Hollow_ — an overgrown copse near Velcairn, marked by twisting roots and standing stones.
    
- **Leadership:**    
    - Harlan Vey — ritualist and experimenter        
    - Lissa Thorn — caretaker of ritual grounds
        
- **Short-Term Goals:** Complete a minor ritual to encourage unusual growth in a sacred grove.
    
- **Long-Term Mission:** Rediscover techniques to communicate with or summon echoes of the Old Gods.
    
- **Methods / Activities:** Fertility rites for plants, geomancy, water pattern manipulation, chanting, meditation.
    
- **Resources:** Hidden grove, rudimentary tools, small circle of followers among farmers and woodsfolk.
    
- **Player-Facing Hooks:** PCs might assist in experiments, investigate strange phenomena, or uncover hints of escalating danger.
    
- **Internal Dynamics:** Elders lead cautiously, apprentices push boundaries; tension over risk-taking.
    
- **Relative Impact:** 1 (local)
