### **Velcairn Trade Consortium** _(Government-Adjacent Merchant Syndicate)_ #Faction
 
**Tier:** 3  
**Description:** Not quite a government, not quite a guild, the Velcairn Trade Consortium is a confederation of merchant houses, banking interests, and shipping lords that collectively dictate policy in [[Velcairn]] and its trade routes. They finance expeditions, levy tariffs, enforce contracts, and maintain a private navy, answering only to their own council of shares. Though they present themselves as defenders of “free commerce,” the Consortium ruthlessly stifles competition and absorbs or destroys rival markets. Their influence extends far beyond the city’s walls, from river caravans to deep-sea ports.  

**Headquarters:** [[The Cinderglass Tower]] 

**Leader:** [[Chairmaster Orren Vellcroft]] – A former shipbreaker turned trade magnate; he understands how to disassemble systems as well as he once dismantled hulls.  

**Known Agents:**
- [[Yllis Dane]] – Trade barrister who settles disputes before they reach court—usually by ensuring one party disappears.
    
- [[Master Tovein Cray]] – Oversees bonded warehouses and tariff offices in Velcairn; devoutly neutral until it threatens Consortium profit.
    
- [[Hedra Willan]] – Mid-tier assessor known for visiting fringe towns before “trade integration” occurs; always takes notes.  
    **Ally:** [[Threadwarden's Guild]] – Their shared interest in regulated and high-margin export has forged a mutually beneficial partnership.  
    
    **Rival:** [[Stonehookers’ Assembly]] – An independent shipwrights' league refusing to join the Consortium and suspected of arming maritime rebels.  
    
    **Short-Term Goal:** Establish a foothold in the coastal port of [[Tarnroth]], securing exclusive docking and customs rights by backing a candidate in the city’s upcoming trade magistrate election.  
    
    **Ultimate Goal:** Replace regional royal and clerical authorities with a stable network of economic vassals loyal to the Consortium alone.
