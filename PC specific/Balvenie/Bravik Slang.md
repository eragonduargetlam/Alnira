## **Bravik Culture: Flavor & Slang**

Bravik is more than a game — it’s tavern gossip, backroom currency, and a little subculture of its own. Here are the touchstones you can drop in for flavor:

- **“Rolling the dread”** → slang for the hope/fear 2d12 roll that sets the round’s mood. (“I rolled the dread low — the whole hand crumbled.”)
    
- **“Cold deck”** → when a player swears their cards are cursed after a losing streak.
    
- **“The Cut”** → shorthand for a single match (best of 3 rounds).
    
- **“Chalk hand”** → someone who always plays safe, never risks a flashy card.
    
- **“Wild bloom”** → someone’s first uncommon card. (“Balvenie just pulled a wild bloom, look at that grin.”)
    
- **“The back table”** → code in taverns for where Bravik is played seriously, with wagers.
    
- **Cards as coin:** Single cards are often traded for drinks, gear, or favors — especially uncommons. Collectors are notorious for “paying too much” if it fills a gap in their set
