
- Trained as a **Runic Logistician**, focusing on enchantment, containment, and probabilistic divination (a perfect segue into his obsession with **Bravik cards**).    
- Known for brilliance, but poor adherence to doctrine — too experimental, too curious about the _why_ instead of the _how_.    
- Left the Arcanostratum under one of the following possible routes:    

---

## **Three Plausible Break Routes**

### **1. The Sanctioned Sabbatical**

- **Framing:** Balvenie earned temporary leave under the pretense of “field research in divinatory sigil applications.”    
- **Reality:** He simply never returned, but maintains the appearance of an ongoing research assignment.    
- **Why It Works:** The military bureaucracy is vast and indifferent — as long as no scandal arises and his stipend papers remain clean, no one looks too closely.    
- **Tone:** Subtle rebellion through red tape; he’s technically not a deserter, just _extremely overdue_.    

---
### **2. The Quiet Discharge**

- **Framing:** During a failed logistics experiment — perhaps a mass teleportation gone awry — Balvenie’s unit suffered casualties.    
- **Outcome:** He took the blame, was quietly discharged to avoid public inquiry.    
- **Why It Works:** The military prefers discretion; no need to punish a valuable mind when the real fault lies in faulty oversight.    
- **Tone:** Guilt-tinged exile; he left believing he’d done enough harm. Collecting Bravik cards became his self-imposed penance — focusing on patterns he could _control_.    

---
### **3. The Vanishing Papertrail**

- **Framing:** Balvenie was part of a classified logistics project that was later erased from official record — intentionally decommissioned or politically inconvenient.    
- **Reality:** He’s a ghost in the system: listed as “unassigned, pending reassignment” for a decade.    
- **Why It Works:** Anyone who checks assumes someone else has his file; no one is motivated to find him.    
- **Tone:** Bureaucratic limbo — he didn’t leave so much as _slip between the lines_.  
    This route also opens future intrigue: someone might “rediscover” his file and drag him back into the machine.
    
---
### **Narrative Leverage**

- **In-World Reputation:**  
    Fellow wizards might recognize his insignia (a stylized circle within a squared sigil — “Geometry of War”) but assume he’s either inactive or attached to intelligence.
    
- **Thematic Echo:**  
    The Arcanostratum mirrors Malel’s legacy — structured, ruthless, but not malevolent. It trains weapons, not thinkers, and Balvenie’s defection embodies the tension between intellect and institution.
    
- **Future Hook:**  
    A current Archmagus-General may want him back — not for punishment, but because his probabilistic theories could be the key to predicting divine anomalies or battlefield rift events.
