## **Node 1 — The Whisper in Velcairn**

- **Timing:** Session 1 in Velcairn (Day 1–2 after arrival).
    
- **Why Here:** The gossip is harmless enough to slip in early. Sets the hook without demanding immediate action.
    
- **Duration:** 1–2 in-game days. Orion can pocket it or chase it right away.
    

---

## **Node 2 — The Fragment Performance**

- **Timing:** 1–2 weeks later.
    
- **Why Here:** Gives Orion time to pursue unrelated adventures or downtime before the fragment resurfaces in public.
    
- **Duration:** One night. The performance leaves ripples in tavern talk for several days.
    

---

## **Node 3 — The Collector’s Boast**

- **Timing:** About 1 month after Node 2.
    
- **Why Here:** By now, enough chatter circulates that collectors and patrons are sniffing out “forbidden works.” The scandal begins to attach itself to names like Serenya and Drome.
    
- **Duration:** 2–3 in-game days if Orion pursues it directly; otherwise it lingers as background rumor.
    

---

## **Node 4 — The Echoed Chorus**

- **Timing:** 2–3 months after Node 3.
    
- **Why Here:** The Ballad’s spread matures into _performance culture_ — whole troupes using it as material. This feels earned after a slow build.
    
- **Duration:** The performance and shutdown happen in one night, but the aftermath (rumors, censorship debates) ripple for weeks.
    

---

## **Node 5 — The Whispered Name**

- **Timing:** 2–3 months after Node 4.
    
- **Why Here:** This is the payoff stage. Enough has spread that old masters feel the need to speak up. It also gives Serenya’s guilt more time to simmer.
    
- **Duration:** A single conversation, but its consequences echo permanently.
    

---

# **Macro Timeline**

- **Start to Finish:** ~6–8 months of in-game time (if played at “city life” pacing with downtime).
    
- **Faster Option:** Compress to ~2–3 months if you want tighter tension.
    
- **Slower Option:** Stretch nodes with “background rumor” so Orion hears of them but doesn’t touch them until later arcs — could last over a year of in-world time.

# **How Orion’s Choices Shift Pace**

- **If Orion pursues aggressively:** Nodes accelerate — fragments, collectors, and censorship all converge within weeks instead of months.
    
- **If Orion avoids or downplays it:** The Ballad spreads on its own, keeping pressure low until it bursts in Node 4 (forcing confrontation).
    
- **If Orion allies with Serenya early:** She feeds him pieces sooner, speeding everything up.
    
- **If Orion sides with Drome or ignores the scandal:** The Harpers escalate quietly, pulling him back in during Node 5 whether he likes it or not.
