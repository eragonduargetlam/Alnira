_Faerie Bard — The Great Refrain Pilgrim_

### **Cultural Origin: The Heshar Tribe**
- **Homeland:** Obsidian mesas of the Sun-Forged Lands. Days baked in heat and mirage, nights cold and echoing.    
- **Ethos:** “_Take by touch._” Combat is ritualized — the goal is dominance through contact, not destruction. To strike without killing proves mastery of restraint and respect for survival.    
- **Purpose of Capture:** A captured warrior serves the new tribe for one year, then joins as kin. Strength flows where skill gathers.    
- **Craft Focus:** The Heshar measure worth by creation — whether tool, melody, or strategy. To craft is to prove the tribe’s endurance.    
- **Philosophical Conflict:** The concept of _taking without touch_ — persuasion, illusion, manipulation — emerged as a dangerous curiosity. The tribe sought to understand it without succumbing to it.    
    - Thus, they sent Orion across the sea to learn the _arts of intangible conquest_ — words, music, and emotion — from the Bard’s College of Luthallein.
        
### **Narrative Position**
- Orion is not a mere bard — he’s a cultural envoy, a living experiment in empathy and control.    
- The College sees him as a curiosity; the Heshar see him as a test case.    
- His pilgrimage, _The Great Refrain_, isn’t only about proving artistry — it’s about discovering whether manipulation can exist without corruption, and whether song can _touch_ the spirit as firmly as hand to flesh.

## 🌟 CLASS FEATURES
### ✦ Make a Scene
- Spend 3 hope: temporarily _Distract_  a target within Close range, giving them a -2 penalty to their difficulty

### ✦ Rally
- **Once per session**, describe how you rally the party and give yourself and each of your allies a Rally Die. At level 1, your Rally die is a d6. A PC can spend their Rally Die to roll it, adding the result to their action roll, reaction roll, damage roll, or to clear a number of Stress equal to the result. At the end of each session, clear all unspent Rally Dice.
- At level 5, your Rally Die increased to a d8
