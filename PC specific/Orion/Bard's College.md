### **The Bardic College of [[Luthallein]]**

- **Location:**  [[Luthallein]] - A **glittering metropolis** of glass libraries, marble terraces, and cliffside amphitheaters overlooking the Crescent Gulf.   
- **Founding Principle:** To cultivate the arts of story, song, and performance into something transcendent: not just entertainment, but living history and cultural power.
    
- **Tradition:** Students either:    
    - **Pilgrimage Path:** Travel incognito, under pseudonyms, gathering stories, songs, and folk wisdom. Success is measured by how these tales are transformed into something enduring.        
    - **Collegiate Path:** Stay within the College, honing craft until producing a “masterpiece” that earns peer and master recognition.

---
### **Structure**

- **Masters’ Circle:** Senior bards who evaluate students’ works. Not purely democratic—favoritism, rivalry, and politics creep in.    
- **Archivists:** Custodians of stories gathered by pilgrims, ensuring oral traditions aren’t lost. Whispered to know “too much” about rulers and nobles.    
- **Instructors:** Vary wildly—some are brilliant mentors, others petty tyrants clinging to reputation.

---
### **Internal Politics**

- **Competition is fierce.** Every performance is both art and power play. Students sabotage each other subtly (or not). Orion’s betrayal fits perfectly here.    
- **Recognition and ownership of work** is murky. Collaborative projects often get twisted—who really gets the credit? This gives you a baked-in source of tension and NPC drama.    
- **Whispered Factionalism:** Not every bard agrees on the “true” purpose of their craft:    
    - _The Preservers_ (art for art’s sake; record and protect traditions).        
    - _The Influencers_ (use art to sway hearts, policy, and rulers).        
    - _The Harpers_ (rumored, hidden, straddling both sides).        

---
### **The Harpers** _(Rumor Only)_

- **Nature:** A clandestine network of bards and storytellers who act as stability-keepers. They don’t seek to dominate, only to prevent collapse.    
- **Methods:** Information gathering, soft propaganda, careful intervention. They prize balance above all.    
- **Secrecy:** Among students, they’re half-legend, half-joke. Among masters, only a few know the truth. Outsiders dismiss them entirely.    
- **Hook for Orion:** His admiration makes sense—he sees them as an escape from the College’s politicking, a higher calling for his art.
