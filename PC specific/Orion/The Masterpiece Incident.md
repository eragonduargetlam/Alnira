### 🎭 **The Masterpiece Incident — Quick Summary**

- Orion and Serenya co-created a revolutionary duet in Luthallein.    
- **Calvessan Drome** claimed Serenya as sole author, erasing Orion’s name for political gain.    
- Serenya accepted the credit, silencing Orion’s protest.    
- Rather than fight corruption, Orion **chose to leave on Refrain**, seeking truth beyond the College’s reach.    
- The stolen piece, renamed **_Elegy of Dawn_**, made Serenya famous and cemented Calvessan’s power.    
- Years later, the resurfacing of **The Ballad of the Silent Star** threatens to expose their lie—and draw Orion home.

### 🎭 **Serenya Valcoran's Perspective — The Masterpiece Incident (Quick Summary)**

- Collaborated with Orion on a groundbreaking duet that blurred love and art.    
- **Calvessan Drome** offered patronage if she accepted sole credit — promising fame and protection.    
- She hesitated, then **chose ambition over honesty**, convincing herself Orion’s ideals would survive without her.    
- Publicly adored as the author of **_Elegy of Dawn_**, privately haunted by guilt and silence.
- Avoided contact after Orion left on Refrain, clinging to success as justification.    
- Now, with **The Ballad of the Silent Star** resurfacing, she fears history—and Orion—will demand the truth she buried.

### 🎭 **Master Calvessan Drome — The Masterpiece Incident (Quick Summary)**

- As Archmaster of Lore, Calvessan saw Orion and Serenya’s duet as a threat to the College’s hierarchy.    
- **Engineered Serenya’s rise** by erasing Orion’s authorship, claiming that “two names divide a legend.”    
- Used the resulting success, **_Elegy of Dawn_**, to cement his faction’s dominance and his image as “Patron of Prodigies.”    
- Suppressed Orion’s drafts in the archives, labeling them _unfinished_ to prevent scrutiny.    
- Views Orion’s departure as a victory—proof that idealists are easily replaced.    
- Now, the resurfacing **Ballad of the Silent Star** jeopardizes his entire legacy, forcing him to decide whether to bury the truth again or destroy those who remember.
