## **The Bardic Pilgrimage**

- **Formal Name:** _The Great Refrain_ (Initiates know to be "out on refrain")
    
- **Tradition:** Initiates leave the College under pseudonyms to walk the world, gathering stories, songs, and truths until they create something worthy of the Masters’ Circle.

- Initiates on Refrain known as Verses
	- "Aye, the tavern’s full of **Verses** this season. Best keep an ear open for fresh tales."
    
- **Identification:**    
    - Pilgrims are taught a series of **subtle sigils and gestures**—hand placements on instruments, coded phrases in verse, small symbols worked into attire.
        
    - Fully trained bards can recognize these cues instantly; common folk rarely notice.
        
    - The tradition ensures Verses don’t interfere in a bard's work but can seek assistance if needed while on Refrain.
