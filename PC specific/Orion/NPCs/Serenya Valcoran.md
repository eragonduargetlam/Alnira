- ## Serenya Valcoran
- **Ancestry:** Elf
    
- **Role at the College:** Orion’s former lover and collaborator, now celebrated as a rising star.
    
- **Appearance:** Tall, graceful, with ink-dark hair bound in braids threaded with silver wire; dresses in flowing silks that catch the light during performances.
    
- **Personality Keywords:** Ambitious, conflicted, magnetic, hesitant.
    
- **The Betrayal:** Allowed Calvessan to promote them as sole author of the masterpiece. Whether from fear, ambition, or misplaced trust, they never defended Orion’s contributions.
    
- **What They’ve Been Doing Since:**    
    - Touring under Calvessan’s sponsorship, dazzling courts and noble halls.
        
    - Secretly plagued by guilt and self-doubt—rumors suggest they’ve written little of substance since the betrayal.
        
    - Torn between loyalty to their mentor and the nagging suspicion they’re little more than his pawn.
        
- **Public Face:** Gifted performer, charismatic, beloved by both peers and audiences.
    
- **Private Truth:** Swept up by Calvessan’s patronage; he advanced her by obscuring Orion’s contribution and attaching her future to his faction.
    
- **Connection to Masterpiece:**    
    - She knows the work was suppressed but was told it was “dangerous,” and went along with Calvessan’s framing to secure advancement.
        
    - She _didn’t orchestrate_ Orion’s betrayal but benefited from it, and now lives with guilt.
        
- **Current Stance:** Torn — she can stay loyal to Calvessan and her success, or face Orion and risk losing everything.
- 
- **Arc Potential:**
    
    - **Redemption:** Serenya admits her complicity, helps Orion trace the piece’s true history, maybe even risks herself against Calvessan.
        
    - **Damnation:** Serenya clings to her place, doubling down on Calvessan’s narrative, choosing power over truth.
