- ## Master Calvessan Drome
- **Ancestry:** Human
    
- **Role:** Archmaster of Lore, keeper of the College’s archives.
    
- **Public Face:** Stern, calculating, the “final authority” on what is worthy of preservation.
    
- **Private Truth:** Actively manipulates access to the archives to control who rises in the College. The masterpiece theft _threatens his order,_ because it unmoors a piece he deliberately buried.
    
- **Connection to Masterpiece:**    
    - He was the one who suppressed the original creator’s work after their death, branding it “unfinished.”        
    - He may have even arranged the “accident” that killed the student, if Orion interprets events that way.
        
- **Current Stance:** Wants the piece destroyed or re-buried before it destabilizes the College’s control.
    
- **At Odds With:** The rumored Harpers, who see the piece as a living tool for subtle influence.
    
- **Appearance:** Silver-streaked auburn hair, meticulously groomed beard, elaborate velvet coats; always performs with subtle flair even in casual speech.
    
- **Personality Keywords:** Charismatic, calculating, self-serving, dismissive.
    
- **The Betrayal:** Claimed Orion’s contributions were trivial, reshaped the narrative to elevate Orion’s lover alone, and used the masterpiece to cement his own prestige as “discoverer of the next great bard.”
    
- **What He’s Been Doing Since:**    
    - Raised Orion’s former lover to prominence as his prized protégé.
        
    - Entrenched his influence at the Masters’ Circle, pushing for recognition as the “Patron of Prodigies.”
        
    - May have made quiet enemies among other masters who resent his favoritism.
