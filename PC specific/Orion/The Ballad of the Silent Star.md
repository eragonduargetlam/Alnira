## **Node 1 — The Whisper in Velcairn**

- **Trigger:** Orion encounters another **Verse** in Velcairn who recognizes his sigils. This bard, half-drunk and eager to gossip, shares rumors.
    
- **Scene:** Over ale or in a crowded square, the bard whispers: _“Someone broke into the College archives. Stole a masterpiece they’d buried — The Ballad of the Silent Star. Now fragments are turning up in taverns and stages all over the city.”_
    
- **Personal Touch:**    
    - Calvessan’s name surfaces indirectly: _“Drome’s archivists are scrambling, and Valcoran? She’s keeping her head high. Hard to rise above such scandal when your patron’s on the hook.”_
        
- **Interpretation Hooks:*    
    - _Chaos_: Songs can’t be caged. This was inevitable.        
    - _Harpers_: Sounds like deliberate release — test material for influence.        
    - _Corruption_: The College isn’t secure; what else have they hidden?        
- **College Response Opportunity:** _None yet — Orion’s just overhearing whispers._

## **Node 2 — The Fragment Performance**

- **Trigger:** In a tavern, theater, or square, Orion hears a **performance** that contains a fragment of the Ballad.
    
- **Scene:** The refrain changes meaning depending on who sings it. Some patrons leave smiling, others unsettled. Orion recognizes this _from his student days._ It should never be out in the wild.
    
- **Personal Touch:**    
    - On the same night, Serenya performs nearby. Her show is dazzling, but _pointedly excludes any fragment._ Locals gossip: _“Valcoran’s loyal to her patron. Or maybe she’s afraid.”_
        
- **Interpretation Hooks:**    
    - _Chaos_: Proof the song is alive, reshaping itself.        
    - _Harpers_: Someone intentionally seeded it here.        
    - _Corruption_: Performers somehow accessed forbidden material.        
- **College Response Opportunity:** A **Friendly Reminder** — a Valcoran protégé warns Orion not to “chase archive dust.”
    

---

## **Node 3 — The Collector’s Boast**

- **Trigger:** Word spreads of a private collector boasting he has an “original student draft” of the Ballad. Orion is invited or hears secondhand.
    
- **Scene:** In the collector’s home, Orion sees the draft — messy, fragile, written in a shaky student hand. The provenance traces back alarmingly close to a **College archivist loyal to Calvessan.**
    
- **Personal Touch:**    
    - The collector insists it was copied in the same year Serenya advanced — implying her rise is linked to this theft.
        
- **Interpretation Hooks:**    
    - _Chaos_: The fragment just circulated by chance.        
    - _Harpers_: A deliberate leak, slipped into a collector’s hands.        
    - _Corruption_: Calvessan’s people profited off betrayal.        
- **College Response Opportunity:** **Official Line** — Drome issues a statement declaring the piece “unfinished nonsense,” trying to quash the scandal.

## **Node 4 — The Echoed Chorus**

- **Trigger:** A bardic troupe stages a new play incorporating entire scenes of the Ballad.
    
- **Scene:** The performance hits like prophecy. The audience splits — some leave inspired, others unsettled, muttering about omens. When pressed, the troupe shrugs: _“We didn’t invent it. Everyone’s doing it.”_
    
- **Personal Touch:**    
    - Mid-performance, **College-affiliated enforcers** arrive to shut it down. They don’t even hide whose orders they follow: _“Drome won’t have this trash staining the College name.”_
        
    - Rumors swirl afterward: Serenya argued privately against the clampdown. _Does Orion believe her rebellion is real, or performative?_
        
- **Interpretation Hooks:**    
    - _Chaos_: The Ballad now belongs to the people.        
    - _Harpers_: A clever mass-influence campaign.        
    - _Corruption_: Censorship at the highest level.
        
- **College Response Opportunity:** **Quiet Test** — Serenya (or an agent) approaches Orion, asking him to interpret/perform a fragment. His choice tells her whether he’s trustworthy, dangerous, or useful.
    

---

## **Node 5 — The Whispered Name**

- **Trigger:** A seasoned bard or retired master seeks Orion out.
    
- **Scene:** In a hushed conversation, they confess: _“The student who wrote the Ballad died on refrain. Valcoran knew them well. Drome buried their work — said it was dangerous.”_ They won’t say if the death was accident or design.
    
- **Personal Touch:**    
    - Serenya’s complicity is now undeniable. She either knew and turned away, or was swept along by Drome’s patronage. Guilt or ambition — Orion must decide.
        
- **Interpretation Hooks:**    
    - _Chaos_: Songs outlive their makers.        
    - _Harpers_: Perhaps the student was one of them.        
    - _Corruption_: Drome silenced a student and erased their art.
        
- **College Response Opportunity:** **Veiled Threat** — an agent corners Orion: _“The College does not lose its treasures twice. Do not let your refrain end here.”_

# **College Response Branch**

(reactions escalate as Orion digs deeper)

1. **Friendly Reminder:** A Valcoran protégé casually warns Orion not to “chase archive dust.”
    
2. **Official Line:** A public statement by Drome labeling the work “unfinished student nonsense.”
    
3. **Quiet Test:** Serenya herself (or a proxy) asks Orion to interpret/perform a fragment. His response determines whether she trusts him.
    
4. **Veiled Threat:** One of Drome’s agents corners Orion: “The College does not lose its treasures twice.”
    
5. **Forked Road:**
    
    - **Serenya’s Invitation:** Private meeting; she offers guilt and a chance at reconciliation.
        
    - **Drome’s Faction:** Offers advancement — if Orion lets the matter drop.

# **Long Game Payoff**

- **If Orion leans Harper:** Serenya can become an ally against Drome, risking herself.
    
- **If Orion leans Corruption:** Serenya becomes tragic, bound to Drome; Drome emerges as the puppetmaster.
    
- **If Orion leans Chaos:** Both Drome and Serenya lose control; the Ballad spreads, Orion becomes an agent of change.
