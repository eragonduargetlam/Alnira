## **The Mycelune Conclave**

_(“The roots whisper where the wind cannot reach.”)_

### **Core Identity**

- **Nature:** A network of secluded druidic groves and fungal circles bound through the **Fungril Mycelium**, a living network of telepathic communion that crosses continents beneath the soil and sea.    
- **Philosophy:** Balance through interconnection. The Conclave believes the world is one organism—its wounds, even divine ones, must be tended through understanding rather than conquest.    
- **Secrecy:** The Conclave avoids confrontation with [[Dawnbearer Synod]] by appearing disorganized — individual groves use distinct local names and deny formal allegiance if questioned.
    
---
### **The Varnhalde Grove — The Lumenhollow Circle**

- **Location:** Hidden in the **Gloamfen Vale**, a moss-drowned woodland where the trees glow faintly from Fungril spores during the new moon.
    
- **Structure:**    
    - **Circle-Keeper:** _Elder Moryss Bolete_, an ancient Fungril hybrid who has partially merged with the mycelium. Speech comes through nearby fungi, never directly.        
    - **Spore-Wardens:** Three druids who maintain the grove’s protective rituals — **Galerina’s potential mentors or peers**.        
    - **The Bloomwatch:** Animal and spirit companions tasked with monitoring incursions, whether divine, infernal, or mortal.
        
- **Function:**    
    - The grove acts as a **listening post** for the Mycelune network. It senses disturbances through the Fungril rootlines — seismic divine ruptures, corrupted soil, or voided growth.        
    - Recently, the network has grown **erratic**, receiving fragmented impressions — “roots cut from beneath,” “light curdled in the marrow,” “voices beneath stone.”        
    - This is their first sign the imbalance is global, not local to Varnhalde.
        
---
### **Communication and Limitations**

- **The Mycelune Communion:**    
    - Messages pass through a trance-like melding into the Fungril network. They travel slowly and lose coherence — meaning the groves are **loosely informed but never omniscient**.        
    - Messages arrive as **impressions**: scent, color, and emotion rather than clear language.        
    - The **Sun-Forged Grove** (across the sea) often responds with curt, clipped impressions — “hold the balance,” “the wound is deep,” “the stone still bleeds.”
    
- **Security:**    
    - Each grove knows only **one upstream and one downstream** contact in the network — an organic chain of compartmentalization that prevents full exposure.        
    - Any grove that betrays secrecy or draws divine attention is quietly **“cut from the thread”** — the Fungril withdraws its connection, and the grove becomes inert.
        
---
### **The Sun-Forged Grove — The Myrroot Bastion**

- **Location:** Beneath the sandstone plateaus of the **Sun-Forged Lands**, where desert and divine flame meet.    
- **Purpose:** Guardian of the **Veiled Core**, a sacred oasis-city protected by mirage wards.    
- **Attitude:** Pragmatic, distrustful, and uncompromising. They see the imbalance as divine entropy — inevitable but manageable if mortals restrain their interference.    
- **Communication:**    
    - They answer other groves only in necessity.        
    - Will not elaborate on the nature of the “central wound” unless a messenger has reached the oasis unaided and been accepted by their **Keeper of Glass Roots**.
        
- **Possible Hooks:**    
    - Clues in Mycelune Communion visions could reveal that the Sun-Forged network node is **distorting** messages — intentionally or as a result of proximity to the divine source of the imbalance.        
    - The grove’s silence could indicate that the oasis city is **built atop** or **around** one of the divine rifts.        

---
### **Relations and Risks**

- **With [[Dawnbearer Synod]]:**    
    - The Synod regards druids as “useful pagans” — tolerated but monitored.        
    - The Conclave’s restraint has spared them persecution, but any overt divine interference (like aiding against a rift) risks drawing the Synod’s inquisitors.        
- **With Other Factions:**    
    - The **Stonehooker’s Assembly** occasionally trades quietly with the Lumenhollow Circle for rare reagents.        
    - The **Threadwarden’s Guild** once bought Fungril-spun silk; this practice stopped after it began showing unpredictable reactive properties in enchanted fabric.
        
- **Current Concern:**    
    - The grove fears the divine vacuum is not just absence but **a spreading decay of purpose** — that the world’s ecosystems and divine currents are unraveling in tandem.
        
---
### **Plot Applications**

- **Galerina’s Role:**    
    - As a Fungril druid, she can commune with other groves but risks psychic contamination from their confusion.        
    - Her grove may send her to **confirm or disprove the theory of global rifts** — she becomes the eyes and spores of the Conclave.        
- **Party Hooks:**    
    - The players could discover that other rifts (in distant lands) produce **different environmental distortions** — drought, metallic rain, fungal overgrowth — each echoing the imbalance differently.        
    - The grove’s silence across the sea may hide a **deeper involvement** — perhaps the Sun-Forged druids are intentionally constraining divine knowledge to prevent mortals from accelerating collapse.
